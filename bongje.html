Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Sewing"?

봉제([bong-je/sewing]) means joining pieces of fabric together with a sewing machine or by hand.

Q2. What is "Sewing work" and "Sewing machine"?

봉제작업([bong-je-jak-eop/sewing work]) is the actual sewing job, and 재봉기([jae-bong-gi/sewing machine]) is the machine used for sewing.

Q3. Why is "Sewing completion" important in EPS-TOPIK?

봉제완성([bong-je-wan-seong/sewing completion]) means the sewing is finished, and it is an important step before inspection and packaging.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Sewing" in Korean - 봉제([bong-je/sewing])

What does "봉제([bong-je/sewing])" mean?

봉제([bong-je/sewing]) means "sewing". It is joining pieces of fabric together using a sewing machine or by hand.

Casual: 봉제야 (bong-je-ya)
Polite: 봉제예요 (bong-je-ye-yo)
Formal: 봉제입니다 (bong-je-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

봉제([bong-je/sewing]) = the core word for sewing.
봉제작업([bong-je-jak-eop/sewing work]) = 봉제 + 작업 (work)
재봉기([jae-bong-gi/sewing machine]) = 재봉 + 기 (machine)
봉제선([bong-je-seon/sewing line]) = 봉제 + 선 (line)
봉제완성([bong-je-wan-seong/sewing completion]) = 봉제 + 완성 (completion)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Daily work: “봉제 했어요? (bong-je hae-sseo-yo?)”
2. Machine: Check the 재봉기([jae-bong-gi/sewing machine]) before starting.
3. Completion: Report 봉제완성([bong-je-wan-seong/sewing completion]) after finishing.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 봉제 했어요? A: bong-je hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you do the sewing?

B: 네, 했어요. B: ne, hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I did.

A: 봉제 잘 됐어요? A: bong-je jal dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Did the sewing go well?

B: 네, 잘 됐어요. B: ne, jal dwae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, it went well. - 봉제([bong-je/sewing])

A: 재봉기 작동해요? A: jae-bong-gi jak-dong-hae-yo?

A: Is the sewing machine working?

B: 네, 작동해요. B: ne, jak-dong-hae-yo.

B: Yes, it is working.

A: 재봉기 이상 있어요? A: jae-bong-gi i-sang i-sseo-yo?

A: Is there a problem with the sewing machine?

B: 아니요, 정상이에요. B: a-ni-yo, jeong-sang-i-e-yo.

B: No, it is normal. - 재봉기([jae-bong-gi/sewing machine])

A: 봉제선 따라 바느질하세요. A: bong-je-seon tta-ra ba-neu-jil-ha-se-yo.

A: Please sew along the sewing line.

B: 네, 알겠습니다. B: ne, al-get-seum-ni-da.

B: Yes, I understand.

A: 봉제선 곧게 나왔어요? A: bong-je-seon got-ge na-wa-sseo-yo?

A: Is the sewing line straight?

B: 네, 곧게 나왔어요. B: ne, got-ge na-wa-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, it is straight. - 봉제선([bong-je-seon/sewing line])

A: 봉제완성 됐어요? A: bong-je-wan-seong dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Is the sewing completed?

B: 네, 됐어요. B: ne, dwae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, it is completed.

A: 봉제완성 보고하세요. A: bong-je-wan-seong bo-go-ha-se-yo.

A: Please report the sewing completion.

B: 네, 보고할게요. B: ne, bo-go-hal-ge-yo.

B: Yes, I will report it. - 봉제완성([bong-je-wan-seong/sewing completion])
