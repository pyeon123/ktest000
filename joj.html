Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Adjustment" in factories?

조절([jo-jeol/adjustment]) means changing speed, temperature, pressure or height to the correct level. It is a core EPS-TOPIK machine operation word.

Q2. What is the difference between 조절 and 조절기?

조절([jo-jeol/adjustment]) is the action of changing a setting. 조절기([jo-jeol-gi/adjuster]) is the device or knob used for the adjustment.

Q3. Why is 속도조절 important?

속도조절([sok-do-jo-jeol/speed adjustment]) changes the machine speed. Correct speed is necessary for product quality and safety.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Adjustment" in Korean - 조절([jo-jeol/adjustment])

What does "조절([jo-jeol/adjustment])" mean?

조절([jo-jeol/adjustment]) means "adjustment". It is the action of changing speed, temperature, pressure or height to the correct level.

Casual: 조절이야 (jo-jeol-i-ya)
Polite: 조절이에요 (jo-jeol-i-e-yo)
Formal: 조절입니다 (jo-jeol-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

조절([jo-jeol/adjustment]) = adjustment process
조절기([jo-jeol-gi/adjuster]) = adjuster / control device
속도조절([sok-do-jo-jeol/speed adjustment]) = speed adjustment
온도조절([on-do-jo-jeol/temperature adjustment]) = temperature adjustment
조절공정([jo-jeol-gong-jeong/adjustment process]) = complete adjustment process

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Asking about process: “오늘 조절 있어요?”
2. Device: “조절기 위치 확인했어요?”
3. Specific type: “속도조절 했어요?”

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 오늘 조절 있어요? A: o-neul jo-jeol i-sseo-yo?

A: Is there adjustment today?

B: 네, 시작 전에 해요. B: ne, si-jak jeon-e hae-yo.

B: Yes, we do it before starting.

A: 속도 확인하세요. A: sok-do hwak-in-ha-se-yo.

A: Please check the speed.

B: 알겠습니다. B: al-get-seum-ni-da.

B: Understood. - 조절([jo-jeol/adjustment])

A: 조절기 위치 확인했어요? A: jo-jeol-gi wi-chi hwak-in-hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you check the position of the adjuster?

B: 네, 확인했어요. B: ne, hwak-in-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I checked.

A: 천천히 돌리세요. A: cheon-cheon-hi dol-li-se-yo.

A: Please turn it slowly.

B: 네, 바로 할게요. B: ne, ba-ro 할-ge-yo.

B: Yes, I’ll do it right away. - 조절기([jo-jeol-gi/adjuster])

A: 속도조절 했어요? A: sok-do-jo-jeol hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you do the speed adjustment?

B: 네, 했어요. B: ne, hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I did.

A: 현재 속도 얼마예요? A: hyeon-jae sok-do eol-ma-ye-yo?

A: What is the current speed?

B: 150입니다. B: baek-o-sip-im-ni-da.

B: It is 150. - 속도조절([sok-do-jo-jeol/speed adjustment])

A: 온도조절 했어요? A: on-do-jo-jeol hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you do the temperature adjustment?

B: 네, 했어요. B: ne, hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I did.

A: 현재 온도 확인하세요. A: hyeon-jae on-do hwak-in-ha-se-yo.

A: Please check the current temperature.

B: 알겠습니다. B: al-get-seum-ni-da.

B: Understood. - 온도조절([on-do-jo-jeol/temperature adjustment])
