Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Inspection"?

검사([geom-sa/inspection]) means inspection or examination of product or parts at factory to find defect and check quality before shipping.

Q2. What is quality and visual inspection?

품질검사([pum-jil-geom-sa/quality inspection]) is checking quality, 외관검사([oe-gwan-geom-sa/visual inspection]) is checking appearance by eyes.

Q3. What is 100% inspection?

전수검사([jeon-su-geom-sa/100% inspection]) means inspecting all products one by one not sampling for important quality control.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Inspection" in Korean - 검사([geom-sa/inspection])

What does "검사([geom-sa/inspection])" mean?

검사([geom-sa/inspection]) means "inspection" or "examination / checking quality".

Casual: 검사야 (geom-sa-ya)
Polite: 검사예요 (geom-sa-ye-yo)
Formal: 검사입니다 (geom-sa-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

검사([geom-sa/inspection]) = 검사([geom-sa/inspection]) itself. 검사하다([geom-sa-ha-da/to inspect]) = 검사 + 하다([ha-da/to do]), 품질검사([pum-jil-geom-sa/quality inspection]) = 품질([pum-jil/quality]) + 검사, 외관검사([oe-gwan-geom-sa/visual]) = 외관([oe-gwan/appearance]) + 검사, 전수검사([jeon-su-geom-sa/100% inspection]) = 전수([jeon-su/all count]) + 검사.

When to Use It

1. Inspection: "검사가 뭐예요? (geom-sa-ga mwo-ye-yo?)"
2. Quality/visual: 품질검사([pum-jil-geom-sa/quality inspection]) is checking quality like QC, 외관검사([oe-gwan-geom-sa/visual inspection]) is checking by 눈([nun/eyes]) like 흠집([heum-jip/scratch]) or 찍힘([jji-kim/dent]).
3. 100%: 전수검사([jeon-su-geom-sa/100% inspection]) is inspecting 전부([jeon-bu/all]) one by one not 샘플검사([saem-peul-geom-sa/sampling inspection]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 검사가 뭐예요? A: geom-sa-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is inspection?

B: 확인하는 거예요. B: hwa-gin-ha-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: Checking.

A: 검사해요? A: geom-sa-hae-yo?

A: Do you inspect?

B: 네, 검사해요. B: ne, geom-sa-hae-yo.

B: Yes, inspect. - 검사([geom-sa/inspection])

A: 품질검사가 뭐예요? A: pum-jil-geom-sa-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is quality inspection?

B: 품질 확인하는 거예요. B: pum-jil hwa-gin-ha-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: Checking quality.

A: 품질검사 했어요? A: pum-jil-geom-sa hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you do QC?

B: 네, 했어요. B: ne, hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, did. - 품질검사([pum-jil-geom-sa/quality inspection])

A: 외관검사가 뭐예요? A: oe-gwan-geom-sa-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is visual inspection?

B: 겉모양 검사하는 거예요. B: geot-mo-yang geom-sa-ha-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: Inspecting appearance.

A: 외관검사 해요? A: oe-gwan-geom-sa hae-yo?

A: Do you do visual inspection?

B: 네, 외관검사해요. B: ne, oe-gwan-geom-sa-hae-yo.

B: Yes, do. - 외관검사([oe-gwan-geom-sa/visual inspection])

A: 전수검사가 뭐예요? A: jeon-su-geom-sa-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is 100% inspection?

B: 전부 검사하는 거예요. B: jeon-bu geom-sa-ha-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: Inspecting all.

A: 전수검사해요? A: jeon-su-geom-sa-hae-yo?

A: Do 100% inspection?

B: 네, 전수검사해요. B: ne, jeon-su-geom-sa-hae-yo.

B: Yes, do. - 전수검사([jeon-su-geom-sa/100% inspection])
