Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "I need help" — 도움이 필요해요 (doumi pilyohaeyo)?

Koreans use it when they need assistance, such as asking for directions, support, or help in daily situations.

Q2. Is "I need help" — 도움이 필요해요 (doumi pilyohaeyo) polite enough?

Yes. "도움이 필요해요 (doumi pilyohaeyo)" is polite and natural. You can also say "도움이 필요해 (doumi pilyohae)" casually.

Q3. What should I say in a formal setting?

You can say "I need help" — 도움이 필요합니다 (doumi pilyohamnida).

Learn More: How to Say "I Need Help" in Korean

What does "도움이 필요해요 (doumi pilyohaeyo)" mean?

도움이 필요해요 means "I need help." It's a polite way to ask for assistance. 도움 (doum) = help/assistance, 이 (i) = subject marker, 필요하다 (pilyohada) = to need → 필요해요 (pilyohaeyo) = polite form. Essential for travel, emergencies, shopping, and daily life when you're lost or need support.

Polite: 도움이 필요해요 (do-um-i pil-yo-hae-yo) - Standard
Formal: 도움이 필요합니다 (do-um-i pil-yo-ham-ni-da) - Staff, official
Casual: 도움이 필요해 (do-um-i pil-yo-hae) - Friends

Grammar Breakdown

도움 (doum) = help, assistance (noun)
이 (i) = subject particle
필요하다 → 필요해요 (pilyohada → pilyohaeyo) = to need → I need (polite)
→ 도움이 필요해요 = Help is needed → I need help.

When to Use It

1. Lost / directions: You don't know the way.
2. Shopping / service: Need assistance.
3. Emergency: Polite request for support.

Real-Life Examples

도움이 필요해요. 길을 잃었어요. do-um-i pil-yo-hae-yo. gil-eul ilh-eoss-eo-yo.

I need help. I'm lost.

저기요, 도움이 필요합니다. jeo-gi-yo, do-um-i pil-yo-ham-ni-da.

Excuse me, I need help. (Formal)

도움이 필요해. 좀 도와줄래? do-um-i pil-yo-hae. jom do-wa-jul-lae?

I need help. Can you help me a bit? (Casual)

무엇을 도와드릴까요? mu-eos-eul do-wa-deu-ril-kka-yo?

How can I help you? (Reply you will hear)

• 길을 모르겠어요 (gireul moreugesseoyo) - I don't know the way
