Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Dormitory"?

기숙사([gi-suk-sa/dormitory]) means dormitory provided by company or factory for workers to live near workplace.

Q2. What is dormitory fee and application?

기숙사비([gi-suk-sa-bi/dormitory fee]) is fee for dormitory, 기숙사신청([gi-suk-sa-sin-cheong/dormitory application]) is applying for dormitory.

Q3. What is check-in and rules?

기숙사입사([gi-suk-sa-ip-sa/moving into dormitory]) is moving into dorm, 기숙사규칙([gi-suk-sa-gyu-chik/dormitory rules]) is rules to follow in dormitory.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Dormitory" in Korean - 기숙사([gi-suk-sa/dormitory])

What does "기숙사([gi-suk-sa/dormitory])" mean?

기숙사([gi-suk-sa/dormitory]) means "dormitory" or "company housing for workers".

Casual: 기숙사야 (gi-suk-sa-ya)
Polite: 기숙사예요 (gi-suk-sa-ye-yo)
Formal: 기숙사입니다 (gi-suk-sa-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

기숙사([gi-suk-sa/dormitory]) = 기숙([gi-suk/boarding]) + 사([sa/house]). 기숙사비([gi-suk-sa-bi/fee]) = 기숙사 + 비([bi/fee]), 기숙사신청([gi-suk-sa-sin-cheong/application]) = 기숙사 + 신청([sin-cheong/application]), 기숙사입사([gi-suk-sa-ip-sa/check-in]) = 기숙사 + 입사([ip-sa/entering]), 기숙사규칙([gi-suk-sa-gyu-chik/rules]) = 기숙사 + 규칙([gyu-chik/rules]).

When to Use It

1. Dormitory: "기숙사가 뭐예요? (gi-suk-sa-ga mwo-ye-yo?)"
2. Fee/application: 기숙사비([gi-suk-sa-bi/dormitory fee]) is fee like 10만원([sip-man-won/100,000 won]), 기숙사신청([gi-suk-sa-sin-cheong/dormitory application]) is applying at 사무실([sa-mu-sil/office]).
3. Check-in/rules: 기숙사입사([gi-suk-sa-ip-sa/moving into dormitory]) is moving in with 짐([jim/baggage]), 기숙사규칙([gi-suk-sa-gyu-chik/dormitory rules]) is rules like 금연([geum-yeon/no smoking]) and 청소당번([cheong-so-dang-beon/cleaning duty]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 기숙사가 뭐예요? A: gi-suk-sa-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is dormitory?

B: 회사 집이에요. B: hoe-sa jip-i-e-yo.

B: It's company housing.

A: 기숙사 있어요? A: gi-suk-sa i-sseo-yo?

A: Is there dormitory?

B: 네, 있어요. B: ne, i-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, exists. - 기숙사([gi-suk-sa/dormitory])

A: 기숙사비가 뭐예요? A: gi-suk-sa-bi-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is dorm fee?

B: 기숙사 비용이에요. B: gi-suk-sa bi-yong-i-e-yo.

B: It's dorm cost.

A: 기숙사비 얼마예요? A: gi-suk-sa-bi eol-ma-ye-yo?

A: How much is dorm fee?

B: 10만원이에요. B: sip-man-won-i-e-yo.

B: 100,000 won. - 기숙사비([gi-suk-sa-bi/dormitory fee])

A: 기숙사입사 했어요? A: gi-suk-sa-ip-sa hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you move into dorm?

B: 네, 입사했어요. B: ne, ip-sa-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, moved in.

A: 기숙사입사 언제 해요? A: gi-suk-sa-ip-sa eon-je hae-yo?

A: When to move in?

B: 내일 입사해요. B: nae-il ip-sa-hae-yo.

B: Move in tomorrow. - 기숙사입사([gi-suk-sa-ip-sa/moving into dormitory])

A: 기숙사규칙이 뭐예요? A: gi-suk-sa-gyu-chik-i mwo-ye-yo?

A: What are dorm rules?

B: 기숙사 규정이에요. B: gi-suk-sa gyu-jeong-i-e-yo.

B: It's dorm regulations.

A: 기숙사규칙 지켜요? A: gi-suk-sa-gyu-chik ji-kyeo-yo?

A: Do you follow dorm rules?

B: 네, 지켜요. B: ne, ji-kyeo-yo.

B: Yes, follow. - 기숙사규칙([gi-suk-sa-gyu-chik/dormitory rules])
