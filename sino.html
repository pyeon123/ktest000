Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Application form"?

신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form]) means application form you fill out at bank, immigration, company for many procedures in Korea.

Q2. What is "Filling out" and "Submission"?

신청서작성([sin-cheong-seo-jak-seong/filling out application form]) is filling out form, 신청서제출([sin-cheong-seo-je-chul/submission of application form]) is submitting form to office.

Q3. What is format and receipt?

신청서양식([sin-cheong-seo-yang-sik/application form format]) is form template you download, 신청서접수([sin-cheong-seo-jeop-su/receipt of application form]) is office receiving your form.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Application form" in Korean - 신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form])

What does "신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form])" mean?

신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form]) means "application form" or "form to apply for something". Form you fill out.

Casual: 신청서야 (sin-cheong-seo-ya)
Polite: 신청서예요 (sin-cheong-seo-ye-yo)
Formal: 신청서입니다 (sin-cheong-seo-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form]) = 신청([sin-cheong/application]) + 서([seo/document/form]). 신청서작성([sin-cheong-seo-jak-seong/filling out]) = 신청서 + 작성([jak-seong/filling out]), 신청서제출([sin-cheong-seo-je-chul/submission]) = 신청서 + 제출([je-chul/submission]), 신청서양식([sin-cheong-seo-yang-sik/format]) = 신청서 + 양식([yang-sik/format]), 신청서접수([sin-cheong-seo-jeop-su/receipt]) = 신청서 + 접수([jeop-su/receipt]).

When to Use It

1. Application form: "신청서가 뭐예요? (sin-cheong-seo-ga mwo-ye-yo?)"
2. Filling/submission: 신청서작성([sin-cheong-seo-jak-seong/filling out application form]) is writing form with 이름([i-reum/name]) and 외국인등록번호([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-beon-ho/alien registration number]) and 서명([seo-myeong/signature]) at 서명란([seo-myeong-ran/signature column]), 신청서제출([sin-cheong-seo-je-chul/submission of application form]) is submitting to 창구([chang-gu/counter]) at 은행([eun-haeng/bank]) or 출입국([chul-ip-guk/immigration]).
3. Format/receipt: 신청서양식([sin-cheong-seo-yang-sik/application form format]) is template you download from 하이코리아([ha-i-ko-ri-a/HiKorea]) website, 신청서접수([sin-cheong-seo-jeop-su/receipt of application form]) is office receiving and giving 접수증([jeop-su-jeung/receipt slip]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 신청서가 뭐예요? A: sin-cheong-seo-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is application form?

B: 신청하는 서류예요. B: sin-cheong-ha-neun seo-ryu-ye-yo.

B: It's form for applying.

A: 신청서 어디 있어요? A: sin-cheong-seo eo-di i-sseo-yo?

A: Where is application form?

B: 은행에 있어요. B: eun-haeng-e i-sseo-yo.

B: At bank. - 신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form])

A: 신청서작성 했어요? A: sin-cheong-seo-jak-seong hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you fill out application form?

B: 네, 작성했어요. B: ne, jak-seong-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, filled out.

A: 신청서작성 어떻게 해요? A: sin-cheong-seo-jak-seong eo-tteo-ke hae-yo?

A: How to fill out application form?

B: 이름 쓰고 서명해요. B: i-reum sseu-go seo-myeong-hae-yo.

B: Write name and sign. - 신청서작성([sin-cheong-seo-jak-seong/filling out application form])

A: 신청서제출 했어요? A: sin-cheong-seo-je-chul hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you submit application form?

B: 네, 제출했어요. B: ne, je-chul-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, submitted.

A: 신청서제출 어디서 해요? A: sin-cheong-seo-je-chul eo-di-seo hae-yo?

A: Where to submit application form?

B: 은행 창구에서 해요. B: eun-haeng chang-gu-e-seo hae-yo.

B: At bank counter. - 신청서제출([sin-cheong-seo-je-chul/submission of application form])

A: 신청서접수 됐어요? A: sin-cheong-seo-jeop-su dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Was application form received?

B: 네, 접수됐어요. B: ne, jeop-su-dwae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, received.

A: 신청서접수 어디서 해요? A: sin-cheong-seo-jeop-su eo-di-seo hae-yo?

A: Where is form received?

B: 은행에서 접수해요. B: eun-haeng-e-seo jeop-su-hae-yo.

B: Received at bank. - 신청서접수([sin-cheong-seo-jeop-su/receipt of application form])
