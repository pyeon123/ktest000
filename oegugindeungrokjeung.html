Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Alien Registration Card"?

외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung/alien registration card]) is ID card for foreigners staying more than 90 days in Korea, very important like Korean resident card.

Q2. What is "Alien registration" and "Number"?

외국인등록([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok/alien registration]) is registering as foreigner, 외국인등록번호([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-beon-ho/alien registration number]) is 13-digit number like Korean resident number.

Q3. What is reissue and loss?

외국인등록증재발급([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-jae-bal-geup/reissue of ARC]) is getting new card when lost or expired, 외국인등록증분실([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-bun-sil/loss of ARC]) is losing card.

What does "외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung/alien registration card])" mean?

외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung/alien registration card]) means "alien registration card" or "ARC ID card for foreigners in Korea". ID card for foreigners staying more than 90 days, very important.

Casual: 외국인등록증이야 (oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-i-ya)
Polite: 외국인등록증이에요 (oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-i-e-yo)
Formal: 외국인등록증입니다 (oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung/alien registration card]) = 외국인([oe-gu-gin/foreigner]) + 등록([deung-rok/registration]) + 증([jeung/card]). 외국인등록([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok/alien registration]) = 외국인 + 등록, 외국인등록번호([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-beon-ho/alien registration number]) = 외국인등록 + 번호([beon-ho/number]), 외국인등록증재발급([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-jae-bal-geup/reissue]) = 외국인등록증 + 재발급([jae-bal-geup/reissue]), 외국인등록증분실([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-bun-sil/loss]) = 외국인등록증 + 분실([bun-sil/loss]).

When to Use It

1. ARC: "외국인등록증 있어요? (oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung i-sseo-yo?)"
2. Registration/number: 외국인등록([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok/alien registration]) is registering within 90일([gu-sip-il/90 days]) at 출입국관리사무소([chul-ip-guk-gwan-li-sa-mu-so/immigration office]), 외국인등록번호([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-beon-ho/alien registration number]) is 13자리([sip-sam-ja-ri/13 digits]) like 990101-1234567.
3. Reissue/loss: 외국인등록증분실([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-bun-sil/loss of ARC]) is losing card, must get 외국인등록증재발급([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung-jae-bal-geup/reissue of ARC]) within 14일([sip-sa-il/14 days]).
