Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Casting"?

주조([ju-jo/casting]) means the process of melting metal and pouring it into a mold. It is a core EPS-TOPIK factory process word.

Q2. What is the difference between 주조 and 주물?

주조([ju-jo/casting]) is the process. 주물([ju-mul/cast product]) is the finished metal piece after it cools.

Q3. Why is 용탕 important for safety?

용탕([yong-tang/molten metal]) is extremely hot. Workers must wear protective gear and follow strict safety rules when handling it.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Casting" in Korean - 주조([ju-jo/casting])

What does "주조([ju-jo/casting])" mean?

주조([ju-jo/casting]) means "casting". It is the process of melting metal and pouring it into a mold to make parts.

Casual: 주조야 (ju-jo-ya)
Polite: 주조예요 (ju-jo-ye-yo)
Formal: 주조입니다 (ju-jo-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

주조([ju-jo/casting]) = casting process
주물([ju-mul/cast product]) = finished cast piece
금형([geum-hyeong/mold]) = mold / die
용탕([yong-tang/molten metal]) = molten metal
주조공정([ju-jo-gong-jeong/casting process]) = complete casting process

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Asking about process: “오늘 주조 있어요?”
2. Finished product: “주물 몇 개 나왔어요?”
3. Safety: “용탕 온도 확인하세요.”

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 오늘 주조 있어요? A: o-neul ju-jo i-sseo-yo?

A: Is there casting today?

B: 네, 오후부터요. B: ne, o-hu-bu-teo-yo.

B: Yes, from the afternoon.

A: 안전 장비 챙기세요. A: an-jeon jang-bi chaeng-gi-se-yo.

A: Please prepare safety gear.

B: 알겠습니다. B: al-get-seum-ni-da.

B: Understood. - 주조([ju-jo/casting])

A: 주물 몇 개 나왔어요? A: ju-mul myeot gae na-wa-sseo-yo?

A: How many cast products came out?

B: 120개입니다. B: baek-i-sip-gae-im-ni-da.

B: 120 pieces.

A: 불량 확인하세요. A: bul-ryang hwak-in-ha-se-yo.

A: Please check for defects.

B: 네, 바로 할게요. B: ne, ba-ro 할-ge-yo.

B: Yes, I’ll do it right away. - 주물([ju-mul/cast product])

A: 금형 준비됐어요? A: geum-hyeong jun-bi-dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Is the mold ready?

B: 네, 청소 끝났어요. B: ne, cheong-so kkeut-na-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, cleaning is finished.

A: 온도 확인하세요. A: on-do hwak-in-ha-se-yo.

A: Please check the temperature.

B: 알겠습니다. B: al-get-seum-ni-da.

B: Understood. - 금형([geum-hyeong/mold])

A: 용탕 온도 괜찮아요? A: yong-tang on-do gwaen-chan-a-yo?

A: Is the molten metal temperature okay?

B: 네, 지금 적당합니다. B: ne, ji-geum jeok-dang-ham-ni-da.

B: Yes, it is just right now.

A: 천천히 부으세요. A: cheon-cheon-hi bu-eo-se-yo.

A: Please pour slowly.

B: 조심하겠습니다. B: jo-sim-ha-get-seum-ni-da.

B: I will be careful. - 용탕([yong-tang/molten metal])
