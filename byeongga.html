Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Sick leave"?

병가([byeong-ga/sick leave]) means official time off because you are sick.

Q2. What is "Medical certificate" and "Sick leave period"?

진단서([jin-dan-seo/medical certificate]) is paper from hospital to prove you are sick, 병가기간([byeong-ga-gi-gan/sick leave period]) is how many days you rest.

Q3. Is sick leave paid?

유급병가([yu-geup-byeong-ga/paid sick leave]) means you get pay during sick leave, and you need 병가신청([byeong-ga-sin-cheong/sick leave application]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Sick Leave" in Korean - 병가([byeong-ga/sick leave])

What does "병가([byeong-ga/sick leave])" mean?

병가([byeong-ga/sick leave]) means "sick leave" or "leave because you are sick". Official time off with hospital proof.

Casual: 병가야 (byeong-ga-ya)
Polite: 병가예요 (byeong-ga-ye-yo)
Formal: 병가입니다 (byeong-ga-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

병가([byeong-ga/sick leave]) = 병([byeong/sick]) + 가([ga/leave]). 병가신청([byeong-ga-sin-cheong/sick leave application]) = 병가 + 신청, 진단서([jin-dan-seo/medical certificate]) = 진단([jin-dan/diagnosis]) + 서([seo/document]), 병가기간([byeong-ga-gi-gan/sick leave period]) = 병가 + 기간, 유급병가([yu-geup-byeong-ga/paid sick leave]) = 유급([yu-geup/paid]) + 병가.

When to Use It

1. Being sick: "오늘 병가 냈어요? (o-neul byeong-ga nae-sseo-yo?)"
2. Proof: You need 진단서([jin-dan-seo/medical certificate]) from 병원([byeong-won/hospital]) for 병가([byeong-ga/sick leave]).
3. Paid or not: 유급병가([yu-geup-byeong-ga/paid sick leave]) means you still get 월급([wol-geup/monthly salary]) during 병가기간([byeong-ga-gi-gan/sick leave period]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 오늘 병가 냈어요? A: o-neul byeong-ga nae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you take sick leave today?

B: 네, 감기 때문에 병가 냈어요. B: ne, gam-gi ttae-mun-e byeong-ga nae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I took sick leave because of cold.

A: 병가 며칠이에요? A: byeong-ga myeot-chil-i-e-yo?

A: How many days is sick leave?

B: 2일이에요. B: i-il-i-e-yo.

B: It's 2 days. - 병가([byeong-ga/sick leave])

A: 병가신청 했어요? A: byeong-ga-sin-cheong hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you apply for sick leave?

B: 네, 신청했어요. B: ne, sin-cheong-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I applied.

A: 병가신청 어디에 해요? A: byeong-ga-sin-cheong eo-di-e hae-yo?

A: Where do you apply for sick leave?

B: 사무실에 해요. B: sa-mu-sil-e hae-yo.

B: I apply at the office. - 병가신청([byeong-ga-sin-cheong/sick leave application])

A: 진단서 있어요? A: jin-dan-seo i-sseo-yo?

A: Do you have medical certificate?

B: 네, 진단서 있어요. B: ne, jin-dan-seo i-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I have medical certificate.

A: 진단서 어디서 받아요? A: jin-dan-seo eo-di-seo bad-a-yo?

A: Where do you get medical certificate?

B: 병원에서 받아요. B: byeong-won-e-seo bad-a-yo.

B: I get it at the hospital. - 진단서([jin-dan-seo/medical certificate])

A: 병가기간이 언제예요? A: byeong-ga-gi-gan-i eon-je-ye-yo?

A: When is sick leave period?

B: 오늘부터 내일까지예요. B: o-neul-bu-teo nae-il-kka-ji-ye-yo.

B: From today to tomorrow.

A: 병가기간 며칠이에요? A: byeong-ga-gi-gan myeot-chil-i-e-yo?

A: How many days is sick leave period?

B: 2일이에요. B: i-il-i-e-yo.

B: It's 2 days. - 병가기간([byeong-ga-gi-gan/sick leave period])
