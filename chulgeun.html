Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Go to work"?

출근([chul-geun/go to work]) means going from home to factory to start work. You check 출근카드([chul-geun-ka-deu/time card]) when you arrive.

Q2. What is "Work start time" and "Way to work"?

출근시간([chul-geun-si-gan/start time]) is official start time, 출근길([chul-geun-gil/way to work]) is road to work which is often crowded in morning.

Q3. How do you say "Late" and "Time card"?

지각([ji-gak/late]) means arriving after 출근시간([chul-geun-si-gan/start time]), 출근카드([chul-geun-ka-deu/time card]) is card you touch for 근태관리([geun-tae-gwal-li/attendance management]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Go to Work" in Korean - 출근([chul-geun/go to work])

What does "출근([chul-geun/go to work])" mean?

출근([chul-geun/go to work]) means "go to work" or "attendance". It is going from home to workplace to start duty, opposite of 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]).

Casual: 출근이야 (chul-geun-i-ya)
Polite: 출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-e-yo)
Formal: 출근입니다 (chul-geun-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

출근([chul-geun/go to work]) = 출([chul/going out]) + 근([geun/work]). 출근시간([chul-geun-si-gan/start time]) = 출근 + 시간([si-gan/time]), 출근길([chul-geun-gil/way to work]) = 출근 + 길([gil/road]), 지각([ji-gak/late]) = 지([ji/late]) + 각([gak/arrival]), 출근카드([chul-geun-ka-deu/time card]) = 출근 + 카드([ka-deu/card]).

When to Use It

1. Morning attendance: "오늘 출근해요? (o-neul chul-geun-hae-yo?)"
2. Time & road: Talking about 출근시간([chul-geun-si-gan/start time]) and 출근길([chul-geun-gil/way to work]) crowded.
3. Attendance check: Checking 출근카드([chul-geun-ka-deu/time card]) and 지각([ji-gak/late]) with 사장님([sa-jang-nim/boss]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 오늘 출근해요? B: 네, 오늘 출근해요. A: o-neul chul-geun-hae-yo? B: ne, o-neul chul-geun-hae-yo.

A: Do you go to work today? B: Yes, I go today.

A: 몇 시에 출근해요? B: 8시에 출근해요. A: myeot si-e chul-geun-hae-yo? B: yeo-deol-si-e chul-geun-hae-yo.

A: What time? B: At 8.

A: 출근길 막혀요? B: 네, 많이 막혀요. A: chul-geun-gil mak-hyeo-yo? B: ne, man-i mak-hyeo-yo.

Is way to work crowded? Yes, very crowded.

A: 오늘 지각했어요? B: 네, 10분 지각했어요. A: o-neul ji-gak-hae-sseo-yo? B: ne, sip-bun ji-gak-hae-sseo-yo.

Were you late? Yes, 10 minutes late.
