Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "Hello" — 안녕하세요 (annyeonghaseyo)?

Most essential versatile greeting, used any time of day, in any situation — meeting coworker, arriving at store. Often with a slight bow, especially at hoesa / work.

Q2. What's the difference between 안녕하세요, 반가워요, and 오랜만이에요?

안녕하세요 (annyeonghaseyo) = polite hello anytime. 반가워요 (bangaweyo) = nice to meet you / glad to see you. 오랜만이에요 (oraenmanieyo) = long time no see after long time apart.

Q3. How do you say "Really nice to meet you" in Korean?

Say 정말 반가워요 (jeongmal bangaweyo) = I'm really glad to see you, or 만나서 반가워요 (mannaseo bangaweyo) = nice to meet you. Use for old classmates dongchang / classmate or family at chuseok / Korean thanksgiving.

Learn More: How to Say "Hello" in Korean

What does "안녕하세요 (annyeonghaseyo)" mean?

안녕하세요 (annyeonghaseyo) means "Hello" — single most essential versatile greeting, works any time of day.

Polite: 안녕하세요 (an-nyeong-ha-se-yo) - standard hello
Warm: 반가워요 (ban-ga-wo-yo) - nice to meet you
Reunion: 오랜만이에요 (o-raen-man-i-e-yo) - long time no see

Grammar Breakdown

안녕 (an-nyeong) = well-being / peace
하세요 (ha-se-yo) = do (polite question)
→ 안녕하세요 (an-nyeong-ha-se-yo) = Are you at peace? = Hello

When to Use It

1. Anytime: Most versatile greeting — store, work, new coworker — slight bow with it.
2. New meeting: After hello — 만나서 반가워요 (mannaseo bangaweyo).
3. Long time: After years apart — chuseok / Korean thanksgiving, class reunions — 오랜만이에요 (oraenmanieyo).

Real-Life Examples

안녕하세요! 처음 뵙겠습니다. (annyeonghaseyo! cheoeum boepgesseumnida.) an-nyeong-ha-se-yo! cheo-eum boep-gess-seum-ni-da.

Hello! Nice to meet you. (Formal)

만나서 반가워요. 저도 반가워요. (mannaseo bangaweyo. jeodo bangaweyo.) man-na-seo ban-ga-wo-yo. jeo-do ban-ga-wo-yo.

Nice to meet you. Me too.

오랜만이에요! 잘 지냈어요? (oraenmanieyo! jal jinaesseoyo?) o-raen-man-i-e-yo! jal ji-naess-eo-yo?

Long time no see! Have you been well?

Similar Expressions

• 안녕 (annyeong) - Hi casual to friends
• 정말 반가워요 (jeongmal bangaweyo) - Really glad to see you
• 잘 지냈어요? (jal jinaesseoyo?) - Have you been well?
