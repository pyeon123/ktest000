Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Money"?

Q2. What is "Wallet" in Korean?

Q3. How do you say "Card"?

Learn More: How to Say "Money" in Korean

What does "돈 (don)" mean?

돈 (don) means "money" – the most common everyday word for money in Korean.

Casual: 돈이야 (don-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 돈이에요 (don-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 돈입니다 (don-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

돈 (don) is a pure Korean word that simply means money or cash.
It is used in almost every daily situation involving payment or shopping.

When to Use It

1. Having money: "돈 있어? (don it-seo?)" (Do you have money?).
2. Wallet: 지갑 (ji-gap) wallet is where people keep cash and cards.
3. Card payment: 카드 (ka-deu) card is widely used instead of cash.

Real-Life Examples

돈 있어? don it-seo?

Do you have money?

지갑 어디 있어? ji-gap eo-di it-seo?

Where is your wallet?

카드로 할게요. ka-deu-ro hal-ge-yo.

I'll pay with a card.

Similar Expressions
