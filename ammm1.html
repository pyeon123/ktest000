Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "I don't like it" — 안 좋아해요 (an joahaeyo)?

When something doesn't appeal to you — food you don't prefer, clothes, ideas. Polite way to express dislike while maintaining harmony.

Q2. What's the difference between 안 좋아해요, 안 좋아해, and 안 좋아합니다?

안 좋아해요 (an joahaeyo) = polite standard. 안 좋아해 (an joahae) = casual with friends. 안 좋아합니다 (an joahamnida) = formal.

Q3. How to suggest an alternative after saying you don't like it?

Say "너무 매워요 (neomu maeweyo) = It's too spicy" and "그럼 다른 거 먹어요 (geureom dareun geo meogeoyo) = Then let's eat something else."

Learn More: How to Say "I Don't Like It" in Korean

What does "안 좋아해요 (an joahaeyo)" mean?

안 좋아해요 (an joahaeyo) means "I don't like it." 안 (an) = not + 좋아해요 (joahaeyo) = like → 안 좋아해요 (an joahaeyo). Polite way to express dislike while maintaining harmony.

Polite: 안 좋아해요 (an jo-a-hae-yo) - Standard polite
Formal: 안 좋아합니다 (an jo-a-ham-ni-da) - Formal
Casual: 안 좋아해 (an jo-a-hae) - Friends, honest

Grammar Breakdown

안 (an) = not
좋아해요 (jo-a-hae-yo) = like (polite)
안 (an) + 좋아해요 (jo-a-hae-yo) → 안 좋아해요 (an jo-a-hae-yo) = not + like → I don't like it (polite)
→ 안 좋아해요 (an jo-a-hae-yo) = I don't like it.

When to Use It

1. Food preferences: Declining food you don't prefer.
2. Feedback: Clothes, ideas, movies.
3. Social harmony: Honest without being harsh.

Real-Life Examples

안 좋아해요. 너무 매워요. (an joahaeyo. neomu maeweyo.) an jo-a-hae-yo. neo-mu mae-wo-yo.

I don't like it. It's too spicy.

안 좋아합니다. 다른 옵션이 더 좋습니다. (an joahamnida. dareun obsyeoni deo josseumnida.) an jo-a-ham-ni-da. da-reun op-syeon-i deo jo-seum-ni-da.

I don't like it. Other options are better. (Formal)

안 좋아해. 너무 시끄러워. (an joahae. neomu sikkeureowo.) an jo-a-hae. neo-mu si-kkeu-reo-wo.

I don't like it. It's too loud. (Casual)

그럼 다른 거 먹어요. (geureom dareun geo meogeoyo.) geu-reom da-reun geo meok-eo-yo.

Then let's eat something else.

Similar Expressions

• 너무 매워요 (neomu maeweyo) - It's too spicy
• 다른 거 먹어요 (dareun geo meogeoyo) - Let's eat something else
• 싫어요 (sireoyo) - I hate it / I don't like it (stronger)
