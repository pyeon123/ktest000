Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "academy" — 학원 (hagwon)?

Koreans use "학원 (hagwon)" for any private after-school institute — such as math, English, art, or music academies — that students attend outside of regular school hours.

Q2. Is "학원이에요 (hagwon-i-eyo)" polite enough?

Yes. "학원이에요 (hagwon-i-eyo)" is polite and natural for everyday conversation. You can also say "학원이야 (hagwon-i-ya)" casually with friends.

Q3. What should I say in a formal setting?

You can say "It is an academy" — 학원입니다 (hagwon-imnida) — when speaking formally, for example to a teacher or in an interview.

Learn More: How to Say "Academy" in Korean

What does "학원 (ha-gwon)" mean?

학원 means "academy" or "cram school." Literally "study institution." 학 (hak) = study/learning, 원 (won) = institution/center. It refers to a private after-school facility Korean students attend for extra academic instruction in subjects like math, English, and science.

Casual: 학원이야 (ha-gwon-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 학원이에요 (ha-gwon-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 학원입니다 (ha-gwon-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

학 (hak) = study, learning
원 (won) = institution, center
→ 학원 = a place built for studying → academy / cram school.

When to Use It

1. Talking about your daily schedule: "학원에 가요" (Go to the academy).
2. Choosing an academy: Comparing math, English, or art academies.
3. Describing school life: Mentioning 선생님 (teacher), 시험 (test), or 숙제 (homework) related to it.

Real-Life Examples

학원에 가요. 오늘 시험이 있어요. ha-gwon-e ga-yo. o-neul si-heom-i iss-eo-yo.

I'm going to the academy. There's a test today.

선생님이 숙제를 많이 주세요. seon-saeng-nim-i suk-je-reul man-i ju-se-yo.

The teacher gives a lot of homework.

학원 버스를 타고 집에 가. ha-gwon beo-seu-reul ta-go jip-e ga.

I take the academy shuttle bus home. (Casual)

좋은 학원입니다. 추천드려요. jo-eun ha-gwon-im-ni-da. chu-cheon-deu-ryeo-yo.

It is a good academy. I recommend it. (Formal)

Similar Expressions

• 학교 (hak-gyo) - School (regular, not private)
• 과외 (gwa-oe) - Private one-on-one tutoring
• 인강 (in-gang) - Online lecture / internet class
