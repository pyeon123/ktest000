📚 Learn: How to Say Academy in Korean

Academy in Korean is 학원 (ha-gwon). This means private academy or cram school, a key part of Korean education culture.

Other essential school words: 선생님 (teacher), 시험 (exam), 숙제 (homework), 버스 (shuttle bus).

What does 학원 (ha-gwon) mean?

학원 means private academy where Korean students study after school until 10 PM for exam prep.

학원에 가요ha-gwon-e ga-yo
