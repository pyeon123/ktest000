Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is the difference between 학원 and 학교?

학교 (hakgyo) is public regular school. 학원 (hagwon) is private after-school academy/cram school for extra study, very common in Korea until 10 PM.

Q2. Why do Koreans say 선생님 with 님?

님 (nim) is honorific suffix showing respect. Always use 선생님 to teachers. Saying just 선생 is rude.

Q3. What is 학원 버스?

Bright yellow shuttle buses that pick up academy students. Iconic symbol of Korean education neighborhoods.

Learn More: How to Say "Academy" in Korean

What does "학원 (hagwon)" mean?

학원 means "Private Academy / Cram School" in Korean. It's the most iconic word for Korean education culture - private after-school institutes where students study math, English, science until 10 PM. Different from 학교 (school).

Academy: 학원 (ha-gwon) - Private cram school
Teacher: 선생님 (seon-saeng-nim) - Teacher with honorific 님
Exam: 시험 (si-heom) - Test / exam
Homework: 숙제 (suk-je) - Assignment

Grammar Breakdown

학원 (hagwon) = academy (學院 - hanja)
학원에 가요 (hagwone gayo) = I go to academy
선생님 (seonsaengnim) = 선생 (teacher) + 님 (honorific)
시험을 보다 (siheomeul boda) = to take a test

When to Use It

1. After-school academy: 학원에 가요
2. Respect to instructor: 선생님
3. School test: 시험이 있어요 (I have a test)
4. Homework: 숙제가 많아요 (Much homework)

Real-Life Examples

오늘 학원 몇 시에 가요? o-neul ha-gwon myeot si-e ga-yo?

What time do you go to academy today?

선생님, 질문 있어요. seon-saeng-nim, jil-mun iss-eo-yo.

Teacher, I have a question.

내일 시험이야. 공부해야 해. nae-il si-heom-i-ya. gong-bu-hae-ya hae.

Tomorrow is exam. I have to study.

학원 버스가 노랗다. ha-gwon beo-seu-ga no-rah-da.

The academy bus is yellow.

Similar Expressions

• 학원 버스 (hagwon beoseu) - Academy shuttle bus
• 과외 (gwaoe) - Private tutoring
