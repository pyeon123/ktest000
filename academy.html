What does 학원 mean?

학원 (hagwon) means private academy. In Korea, most students go to hagwon after regular school to study math, English, and science until 10 PM. It's a core part of Korean education culture.

How to use school words in Korean

Learn 5 essential school words: 학원 (academy), 선생님 (teacher), 시험 (exam), 숙제 (homework), 버스 (shuttle bus).

Polite: 학원이에요 (ha-gwon-i-e-yo)
Casual: 학원이야 (ha-gwon-i-ya)
