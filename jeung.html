Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Alien Registration Card"?

외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung/alien registration card]) is ID card for foreigners staying in Korea over 90 days, like resident card, very important for daily life.

Q2. What is alien registration number?

외국인등록번호([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-beon-ho/alien registration number]) is 13-digit number like Korean resident number, needed for bank, phone, hospital.

Q3. When to get alien card?

외국인등록증발급([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung-bal-geup/alien card issuance]) must be done within 90 days after entering Korea at immigration office.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Alien Registration Card" in Korean - 외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung/alien registration card])

What does "외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung/alien registration card])" mean?

외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung/alien registration card]) means "alien registration card". It is ID card for foreigners staying over 90 days, needed for daily life.

Casual: 외국인등록증이야 (oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung-i-ya)
Polite: 외국인등록증이에요 (oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung-i-e-yo)
Formal: 외국인등록증입니다 (oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung/alien registration card]) = 외국인 + 등록증 (foreigner + registration card)
외국인등록([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok/alien registration]) = 외국인 + 등록 (foreigner + registration)
외국인등록번호([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-beon-ho/alien registration number]) = 외국인등록 + 번호 (alien registration + number)
외국인등록증발급([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung-bal-geup/alien card issuance]) = 외국인등록증 + 발급 (alien card + issuance)
외국인등록증재발급([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung-jae-bal-geup/alien card reissuance]) = 외국인등록증 + 재발급 (alien card + reissuance)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. ID Card: “외국인등록증 있어요? (oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung i-sseo-yo?)”
2. Number: 외국인등록번호([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-beon-ho/alien registration number]) is 13-digit ID number.
3. Issuance: 외국인등록증발급([oe-gu-gin-deung-nok-jeung-bal-geup/alien card issuance]) within 90 days after entry.
