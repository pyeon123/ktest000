Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "I'm early" — 일찍 왔어요 (iljjik wasseoyo)?

Koreans use it when they arrive before the expected time and want to let others know politely.

Q2. Is "I'm early" — 일찍 왔어요 (iljjik wasseoyo) polite enough?

Yes. "일찍 왔어요 (iljjik wasseoyo)" is polite and natural. You can also say "일찍 왔어 (iljjik wasseo)" casually.

Q3. What should I say in a formal setting?

You can say "I'm early" — 일찍 왔습니다 (iljjik wasseumnida).

Learn More: How to Say "I'm Early" in Korean

What does "일찍 왔어요 (iljjik wasseoyo)" mean?

일찍 왔어요 means "I'm early" or "I came early." It's a polite way to say you arrived before the expected time. 일찍 (iljjik) means "early," and 왔어요 (wasseoyo) comes from 오다 (oda, to come) in past tense. It's positive and shows respect for the appointment.

Polite: 일찍 왔어요 (il-jjik wass-eo-yo) - Most common
Formal: 일찍 왔습니다 (il-jjik wass-seum-ni-da) - Business, official
Casual: 일찍 왔어 (il-jjik wass-eo) - Friends, family

Grammar Breakdown

일찍 (iljjik) = early
오다 → 왔어요 (oda → wasseoyo) = to come → came (past polite)
→ 일찍 왔어요 = I came early / I'm early.

When to Use It

1. Before appointments: Arriving 10-15 minutes early at a cafe or office.
2. At work / school: Letting others know you're ready.
3. For travel: At the airport or station early.

In Korean culture, arriving early is seen as diligent and respectful. It's always a good impression.

Real-Life Examples

일찍 왔어요. 천천히 오세요. il-jjik wass-eo-yo. cheon-cheon-hi o-se-yo.

I'm early. Please come slowly.

일찍 왔습니다. 회의 준비 다 됐습니다. il-jjik wass-seum-ni-da. hoe-ui jun-bi da dwaess-seum-ni-da.

I'm early. The meeting preparation is all done. (Formal)

약속보다 일찍 왔어. 카페에서 기다릴게. yak-sok-bo-da il-jjik wass-eo. ka-pe-e-seo gi-da-ril-ge.

I came earlier than our appointment. I'll wait at the cafe. (Casual)

일찍 와서 좋네요. il-jjik wa-seo jo-ne-yo.

It's good you came early.

Similar Expressions

• 미리 왔어요 (miri wasseoyo) - I came in advance
• 조금 일찍 도착했어요 (jogeum iljjik dochakhaesseoyo) - I arrived a little early
• 시간 남아서 기다리고 있어요 (sigan namaseo gidarigo isseoyo) - I have time left, so I'm waiting
