Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "go to work" — 출근 (chul-geun)?

Koreans use "출근 (chul-geun)" for arriving at or heading to work to begin a shift — a key term for describing daily attendance and often used in the EPS-TOPIK exam.

Q2. Is "출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-e-yo)" polite enough?

Yes. "출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-e-yo)" is polite and natural for everyday conversation. You can also say "출근이야 (chul-geun-i-ya)" casually with close friends.

Q3. What should I say in a formal setting?

You can say "I am going to work now" — 지금 출근입니다 (ji-geum chul-geun-im-ni-da) — when speaking formally, for example to a manager or during a workplace interview.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Go to Work" in Korean

What does "출근 (chul-geun)" mean?

출근 (chul-geun) means "go to work" or "clock in." Literally "leave to work." 출 (chul) = leave/go out, 근 (geun) = work/duty. It refers to arriving at your workplace to begin a shift — one of the most basic words in EPS-TOPIK workplace attendance vocabulary.

Casual: 출근이야 (chul-geun-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 출근입니다 (chul-geun-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

출 (chul) = go out, leave
근 (geun) = work, duty
→ 출근 (chul-geun) = leaving to begin work → go to work / clock in.

When to Use It

1. Talking about your schedule: "출근 시간 (chul-geun si-gan) 시간이 언제예요? (eon-je-ye-yo?)" (What time do you start work?).
2. Explaining lateness: Being careful not to 지각 (ji-gak) (be late) when you 출근 (chul-geun).
3. Describing attendance: Mentioning 결근 (gyeol-geun) (absence), 조퇴 (jo-toe) (leaving early), or 출근카드 (chul-geun-ka-deu) (time card) related to it.

Similar Expressions

• 퇴근 (toe-geun) - Leave work (the opposite of 출근 (chul-geun), finishing a shift)
• 등교 (deung-gyo) - Go to school (the school equivalent of 출근 (chul-geun))
• 야근 (ya-geun) - Night work, working late beyond regular hours

Real-Life Examples

매일 아침 8시에 출근해요. mae-il a-chim yeo-deol-si-e chul-geun-hae-yo.

I go to work at 8 every morning.

버스를 놓쳐서 지각했어요. beo-seu-reul no-chyeo-seo ji-ga-kae-sseo-yo.

I missed the bus, so I was late for work.

몸이 안 좋아서 조퇴했어. mo-mi an jo-a-seo jo-toe-hae-sseo.

I felt unwell, so I left work early. (Casual)

매일 출근카드를 확인합니다. mae-il chul-geun-ka-deu-reul hwa-gin-ham-ni-da.

I check my time card every day. (Formal)
