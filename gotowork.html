Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Go to work"?

출근 (chul-geun) go to work is the standard word for arriving at or heading to your workplace to start a shift.

Q2. What is "Being late" in Korean?

지각 (ji-gak) being late refers to arriving at work after your shift was supposed to start.

Q3. How do you say "Leave early" and "Time card"?

조퇴 (jo-toe) leave early and 출근카드 (chul-geun-ka-deu) time card are essential words for daily workplace attendance.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Go to Work" in Korean

What does "출근 (chul-geun)" mean?

출근 (chul-geun) means "go to work" or "clock in." It is the standard EPS-TOPIK word for arriving at your workplace to begin a shift.

Casual: 출근이야 (chul-geun-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 출근입니다 (chul-geun-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

출근 (chul-geun) is made of two parts — 출 (leave/go out) + 근 (work/duty). It is used across all Korean workplaces to describe starting a shift.

When to Use It

1. Talking about your schedule: "출근 시간이 언제예요? (chul-geun si-gan-i eon-je-ye-yo)" (What time do you start work?).
2. Explaining attendance: Being careful not to 지각 (ji-gak) be late when you 출근.
3. Daily factory life: Mentioning 결근 (gyeol-geun) absence, 조퇴 (jo-toe) leaving early, or 출근카드 (chul-geun-ka-deu) time card.

Real-Life Examples

매일 아침 8시에 출근해요. mae-il a-chim yeo-deol-si-e chul-geun-hae-yo.

I go to work at 8 every morning.

버스를 놓쳐서 지각했어요. beo-seu-reul no-chyeo-seo ji-ga-kae-sseo-yo.

I missed the bus, so I was late for work.

매일 출근카드를 확인합니다. mae-il chul-geun-ka-deu-reul hwa-gin-ham-ni-da.

I check my time card every day.

Similar Expressions

• 퇴근 (toe-geun) leave work
• 지각 (ji-gak) being late
• 결근 (gyeol-geun) absence
• 조퇴 (jo-toe) leaving early
