Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Severance pay"?

퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]) is money given when worker leaves company after working more than 1 year, calculated by average wage x years worked.

Q2. How to calculate severance pay?

퇴직금계산([toe-jik-geum-gye-san/severance pay calculation]) = average daily wage x 30 days x years worked. Must work over 1 year.

Q3. When is severance pay paid?

퇴직금지급([toe-jik-geum-ji-geup/severance pay payment]) must be within 14 days after leaving company by law.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Severance pay" in Korean - 퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay])

What does "퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay])" mean?

퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]) means "severance pay". It is money given when worker leaves after working more than 1 year.

Casual: 퇴직금이야 (toe-jik-geum-i-ya)
Polite: 퇴직금이에요 (toe-jik-geum-i-e-yo)
Formal: 퇴직금입니다 (toe-jik-geum-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]) = 퇴직 + 금 (retirement + money)
퇴직금계산([toe-jik-geum-gye-san/severance pay calculation]) = 퇴직금 + 계산 (severance pay + calculation)
퇴직금지급([toe-jik-geum-ji-geup/severance pay payment]) = 퇴직금 + 지급 (severance pay + payment)
퇴직금정산([toe-jik-geum-jeong-san/severance pay settlement]) = 퇴직금 + 정산 (severance pay + settlement)
퇴직금제도([toe-jik-geum-je-do/severance pay system]) = 퇴직금 + 제도 (severance pay + system)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Leaving: “퇴직금 받았어요? (toe-jik-geum ba-da-sseo-yo?)”
2. Calculation: 평균임금 x 30일 x 근속년수 = 퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]).
3. Payment: Must be paid within 14 days after leaving by law.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 퇴직금 받았어요? A: toe-jik-geum ba-da-sseo-yo?

A: Did you receive severance pay?

B: 네, 받았어요. B: ne, ba-da-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I received it.

A: 퇴직금 얼마예요? A: toe-jik-geum eol-ma-ye-yo?

A: How much is severance pay?

B: 300만 원이에요. B: 300-man won-i-e-yo.

B: It is 3,000,000 won. - 퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay])

A: 퇴직금계산 어떻게 해요? A: toe-jik-geum-gye-san eo-ttae-ke hae-yo?

B: 평균임금으로 계산해요. B: pyeong-gyun-im-geum-eu-ro gye-san-hae-yo.

A: 퇴직금지급 언제예요? A: toe-jik-geum-ji-geup eon-je-ye-yo?

A: When is severance pay payment?

B: 14일 이내예요. B: 14-il i-nae-ye-yo.
