What does "사랑해요 (saranghaeyo)" mean?

사랑해요 means "I love you". It's the standard polite romantic expression used between couples.

Polite: 사랑해요 (sa-rang-hae-yo) - Standard romantic
Formal: 사랑합니다 (sa-rang-ham-ni-da) - For proposals
Casual: 사랑해 (sa-rang-hae) - Intimate

Grammar Breakdown

사랑 (sarang) = love (noun)

하다 (hada) = to do (verb)

해요 (haeyo) = polite present ending

합니다 (hamnida) = formal ending

→ 사랑 + 해요 = 사랑해요 = I love you

When to Use It

1. Daily dating: Goodnight, texting, dates

2. Growing feelings: 너무 좋아해 for early dating

3. Serious: 사랑합니다 for proposals

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. 사랑해요 vs 사랑해 차이?
A: 사랑해요 is polite standard, 사랑해 is intimate casual.

Q2. 사랑합니다는 언제?
A: Formal proposals and very serious confessions.

Q3. 너무 좋아해로 고백?
A: Yes, sweet lighter confession perfect for early dating.
