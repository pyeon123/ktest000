Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Work / Task"?

작업([jak-eop/work]) means the actual task or job you do at a factory or workplace.

Q2. What is "Workplace" and "Work clothes"?

작업장([jak-eop-jang/workplace]) is the place where you work, and 작업복([jak-eop-bok/work clothes]) is the uniform you wear for safety.

Q3. Why is "Safe work" important in EPS-TOPIK?

안전작업([an-jeon-jak-eop/safe work]) is a key word in EPS-TOPIK textbooks because factories always emphasize safety during 작업([jak-eop/work]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Work / Task" in Korean - 작업([jak-eop/work])

What does "작업([jak-eop/work])" mean?

작업([jak-eop/work]) means "work / task / operation". It is the actual job you do in a factory or workplace.

Casual: 작업이야 (jak-eop-i-ya)
Polite: 작업이에요 (jak-eop-i-e-yo)
Formal: 작업입니다 (jak-eop-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

작업([jak-eop/work]) = the core word for task.
작업장([jak-eop-jang/workplace]) = 작업 + 장 (place)
작업복([jak-eop-bok/work clothes]) = 작업 + 복 (clothes)
작업시간([jak-eop-si-gan/working hours]) = 작업 + 시간 (time)
안전작업([an-jeon-jak-eop/safe work]) = 안전 (safety) + 작업

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Daily task: “오늘 작업 뭐예요? (o-neul jak-eop mwo-ye-yo?)”
2. Location: Go to 작업장([jak-eop-jang/workplace]) after changing into 작업복([jak-eop-bok/work clothes]).
3. Safety: Always do 안전작업([an-jeon-jak-eop/safe work]) during 작업시간([jak-eop-si-gan/working hours]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 오늘 작업 뭐예요? A: o-neul jak-eop mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is today's work?

B: 조립 작업이에요. B: jo-rip jak-eop-i-e-yo.

B: It is assembly work.

A: 작업 끝났어요? A: jak-eop kkeut-na-sseo-yo?

A: Is the work finished?

B: 네, 끝났어요. B: ne, kkeut-na-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, it is finished. - 작업([jak-eop/work])

A: 작업장 어디예요? A: jak-eop-jang eo-di-ye-yo?

A: Where is the workplace?

B: 2층이에요. B: i-cheung-i-e-yo.

B: It is on the 2nd floor.

A: 작업장에 들어가요? A: jak-eop-jang-e deu-reo-ga-yo?

A: Do we enter the workplace?

B: 네, 들어가요. B: ne, deu-reo-ga-yo.

B: Yes, we enter. - 작업장([jak-eop-jang/workplace])

A: 작업복 입었어요? A: jak-eop-bok i-beo-sseo-yo?

A: Did you put on work clothes?

B: 네, 입었어요. B: ne, i-beo-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I put them on.

A: 작업복 어디에 있어요? A: jak-eop-bok eo-di-e i-sseo-yo?

A: Where are the work clothes?

B: 락커에 있어요. B: rak-keo-e i-sseo-yo.

B: They are in the locker. - 작업복([jak-eop-bok/work clothes])

A: 안전작업 하세요. A: an-jeon-jak-eop ha-se-yo.

A: Please do safe work.

B: 네, 알겠습니다. B: ne, al-get-seum-ni-da.

B: Yes, I understand.

A: 안전작업 중요해요? A: an-jeon-jak-eop jung-yo-hae-yo?

A: Is safe work important?

B: 네, 아주 중요해요. B: ne, a-ju jung-yo-hae-yo.

B: Yes, it is very important. - 안전작업([an-jeon-jak-eop/safe work])
