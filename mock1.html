    About EPS-TOPIK Mock Test #1

    This free EPS-TOPIK practice test has 20 listening questions and 20 reading questions, 40 in total. Test #1 focuses on greetings, work basics, and safety signs. Every question is shown in Korean, romanized pronunciation, and English, so beginners can follow along.

    After each answer you see right away if it was correct. A yellow Study button then opens a free lesson about the same topic on K-Free.

    What you will practice in Test #1

    • Greetings and daily questions: hello, what time it is, the weather, prices, and where the restroom is
    • Work conversations: arrival time, overtime, salary day, being late, and reporting a broken machine
    • Safety: helmets, gloves, reflective vests, warning signs, and first aid
    • Workplace words and tools: scissors, pliers, excavator, work clothes, and the workplace
    • Reading notices: clinic hours, payslips, staff lounge rules, and social insurance
