EPS-TOPIK test practice: the word 가위 (gawi)

This free EPS-TOPIK test page (also written EPSTOPIK test or EPS TOPIK test) helps you practice one Korean word at a time. Word questions like these are part of EPS-TOPIK test practice, and the same word shows up in listening, reading, and vocabulary questions.

가위 (gawi) means scissors. It is a tool with two blades that you use to cut paper, cloth, thread, and other thin things. Tool words like this appear often in EPS-TOPIK practice questions about the workplace.

Pronunciation

Say ga-wi. It has two syllables: 가 (ga) + 위 (wi).

Example sentences

가위로 종이를 잘라요.gawiro jongireul jallayo.I cut paper with scissors.
가위 좀 주세요.gawi jom juseyo.Please give me the scissors.
이 가위는 천을 자를 때 써요.i gawineun cheoneul jareul ttae sseoyo.I use these scissors when cutting cloth.
