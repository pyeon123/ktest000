Q1. How do Koreans say "Livestock industry"?

Q2. What is livestock barn?

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Livestock industry" in Korean - 축산업([chuk-san-eop/livestock industry])

What does "축산업([chuk-san-eop/livestock industry])" mean?

축산업([chuk-san-eop/livestock industry]) means "livestock industry". It is livestock industry like industry raising animals like cows pigs.

Casual: 축산업이야 (chuk-san-eop-i-ya)
Polite: 축산업이에요 (chuk-sa-neo-bi-e-yo)
Formal: 축산업입니다 (chuk-sa-neo-bim-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

축산업([chuk-san-eop/livestock industry]) = 축산업 (livestock industry)
축사([chuk-sa/livestock barn]) = 축 + 사 (livestock + barn)
축산 농가([chuk-san nong-ga/livestock farm household]) = 축산 + 농가 (livestock + farm household)
축산물([chuk-san-mul/livestock products]) = 축산 + 물 (livestock + products)
축산 농장([chuk-san nong-jang/livestock farm]) = 축산 + 농장 (livestock + farm)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Industry: “축산업이 뭐예요? (chuk-san-eop-i mwo-ye-yo?)”
2. Building: 축사([chuk-sa/livestock barn]) livestock barn.
3. Household: 축산 농가([chuk-san nong-ga/livestock farm household]) livestock farm household.
