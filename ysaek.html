Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Dyeing"?

염색([yeom-saek/dyeing]) means putting color into fabric with chemical water at textile factory. White fabric goes into tank with blue water to get color. Must wear safety gloves and mask.

Q2. What is dyeing machine and process?

염색기([yeom-saek-gi/dyeing machine]) is big drum machine that puts color into fabric. 염색공정([yeom-saek-gong-jeong/dyeing process]) order is fabric prep -> dye mixing -> dyeing -> washing -> dehydration -> drying.

Q3. What is dyeing work and safety?

염색작업([yeom-saek-ja-geop/dyeing work]) uses chemicals. Safety: wear gloves and mask, ventilation, check color evenness and defects, keep temperature 60 degrees.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Dyeing" in Korean - 염색([yeom-saek/dyeing])

What does "염색([yeom-saek/dyeing])" mean?

염색([yeom-saek/dyeing]) means "dyeing" or "coloring fabric / putting color into cloth".

Casual: 염색이야 (yeom-saek-i-ya)
Polite: 염색이에요 (yeom-saek-i-e-yo)
Formal: 염색입니다 (yeom-saek-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

염색([yeom-saek/dyeing]) = 염([yeom/dye]) + 색([saek/color]). 염색하다([yeom-saek-ha-da/to dye]) = 염색 + 하다([ha-da/to do]), 염색기([yeom-saek-gi/machine]) = 염색 + 기([gi/machine]), 염색공정([yeom-saek-gong-jeong/process]) = 염색 + 공정([gong-jeong/process]), 염색작업([yeom-saek-ja-geop/work]) = 염색 + 작업([ja-geop/work]).

When to Use It

1. Dyeing: "염색이 뭐예요? (yeom-saek-i mwo-ye-yo?)"
2. Machine: 염색기([yeom-saek-gi/dyeing machine]) is big drum machine that puts color into fabric.
3. Process/Work: 염색공정([yeom-saek-gong-jeong/dyeing process]) order is 원단준비([won-dan-jun-bi/fabric prep]) -> 염료배합([yeom-ryo-bae-hap/dye mixing]) -> 염색([yeom-saek/dyeing]) -> 수세([su-se/washing)] -> 탈수([tal-su/dehydration]) -> 건조([geon-jo/drying]), 염색작업([yeom-saek-ja-geop/dyeing work]) needs 장갑([jang-gap/gloves]) and 마스크([ma-seu-keu/mask]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 염색이 뭐예요? A: yeom-saek-i mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is dyeing?

B: 천에 색 넣는 거예요. B: cheon-e saek neo-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: Putting color into cloth.

A: 어디에서 염색해요? A: eo-di-e-seo yeom-saek-hae-yo?

A: Where do you dye?

B: 섬유공장에서 염색해요. B: seom-yu-gong-jang-e-seo yeom-saek-hae-yo.

B: Dye at textile factory. - 염색([yeom-saek/dyeing])

A: 무슨 색으로 염색해요? A: mu-seun sae-geu-ro yeom-saek-hae-yo?

A: What color do you dye?

B: 검은색으로 염색해요. B: geom-eun-sae-geu-ro yeom-saek-hae-yo.

B: Dye black.

A: 염색기 온도가 몇 도예요? A: yeom-saek-gi on-do-ga myeot do-ye-yo?

A: What is machine temp?

B: 60도예요. B: yuk-sip do-ye-yo.

B: 60 degrees. - 염색기([yeom-saek-gi/dyeing machine])

A: 염색공정이 뭐예요? A: yeom-saek-gong-jeong-i mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is dyeing process?

B: 염색하는 순서예요. B: yeom-saek-ha-neun sun-seo-ye-yo.

B: Steps of dyeing.

A: 염색공정에서 제일 중요한 게 뭐예요? A: yeom-saek-gong-jeong-e-seo je-il jung-yo-han ge mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is most important in dyeing process?

B: 온도와 시간이에요. B: on-do-wa si-gan-i-e-yo.

B: Temperature and time. - 염색공정([yeom-saek-gong-jeong/dyeing process])

A: 염색작업이 뭐예요? A: yeom-saek-ja-geop-i mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is dyeing work?

B: 염색하는 작업이에요. B: yeom-saek-ha-neun ja-geop-i-e-yo.

B: Work of dyeing.

A: 염색작업할 때 조심할 게 뭐예요? A: yeom-saek-ja-geop-hal ttae jo-sim-hal ge mwo-ye-yo?

A: What to be careful during dyeing work?

B: 장갑과 마스크를 꼭 써야 해요. B: jang-gap-gwa ma-seu-keu-reul kkok sseo-ya hae-yo.

B: Must wear gloves and mask. - 염색작업([yeom-saek-ja-geop/dyeing work])
