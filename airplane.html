How to Say Airplane in Korean?

Airplane in Korean is 비행기 (bi-haeng-gi). This is one of the most essential travel words for beginners.

Formal: 비행기는... (bi-haeng-gi-neun...)
Casual: 비행기야 (bi-haeng-gi-ya)

Essential Travel Vocabulary with Airplane

Koreans love traveling. When you travel, you will hear these words together at the airport.

공항 (gong-hang) - Airport gong-hang

Example: 인천 공항에 가요 (in-cheon gong-hang-e ga-yo) - I'm going to Incheon Airport.

비행기 표 / 티켓 - Airplane Ticket bi-haeng-gi pyo / ti-ket

Example: 비행기 표 예약했어? (bi-haeng-gi pyo ye-yak-haet-seo?) - Did you book the airplane ticket?

탑승 (tab-seung) - Boarding tab-seung

Common announcement: 비행기 탑승을 시작합니다 (bi-haeng-gi tab-seung-eul si-jak-ham-ni-da) - Now boarding the airplane.

Learn More: Real-Life Situations

At Incheon Airport, you need to know these phrases:

• 출국심사 (chul-guk-sim-sa) - Immigration / departure screening
• 보안검색 (bo-an-geom-saek) - Security check
• 수하물 (su-ha-mul) - Luggage / baggage

Useful Pattern: 비행기 몇 시야? (bi-haeng-gi myeot si-ya?) - What time is the flight?
Answer: 오후 3시야 (o-hu se-si-ya) - It's at 3 PM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Airplane"?

비행기 (bi-haeng-gi) is the most common word.

Q2. What is "Airport" in Korean?

공항 (gong-hang) means airport.

Q3. How do you say "Flight"?

항공편 (hang-gong-pyeon) or 비행 (bi-haeng) is used for flight.
