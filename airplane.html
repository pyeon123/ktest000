Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Airplane"?

비행기 (bi-haeng-gi) airplane is the most common everyday word.

Q2. What is "Airport" in Korean?

공항 (gong-hang) airport. Incheon Airport is often called 인천공항 (in-cheon gong-hang).

Q3. How do you say "Ticket" for a flight?

티켓 (ti-ket) ticket or 항공권 (hang-gong-gwon) flight ticket is commonly used.

Learn More: How to Say "Airplane" in Korean

What does "비행기 (bi-haeng-gi)" mean?

비행기 (bi-haeng-gi) means "airplane". Literally “flying-going-machine.” 비 (bi) = fly, 행 (haeng) = go/travel, 기 (gi) = machine/vehicle.

Casual: 비행기야 (bi-haeng-gi-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 비행기예요 (bi-haeng-gi-ye-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 비행기입니다 (bi-haeng-gi-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

비 (bi) = fly / flying
행 (haeng) = go / travel
기 (gi) = machine / vehicle
→ 비행기 (bi-haeng-gi) = flying vehicle → airplane.

When to Use It

1. Talking about travel: "비행기 탈 거예요 (bi-haeng-gi tal geo-ye-yo)" (I’m going to take a plane).
2. At the airport: Asking about departure or boarding.
3. Making plans: Discussing flights with 공항 (gong-hang) airport or 티켓 (ti-ket) ticket.

Real-Life Examples

비행기 몇 시에 출발해요? bi-haeng-gi myeot si-e chul-bal-hae-yo?

What time does the airplane depart?

공항에서 비행기를 기다려요. gong-hang-e-seo bi-haeng-gi-reul gi-da-ryeo-yo.

I’m waiting for the airplane at the airport.

티켓을 미리 예약했어요. ti-ket-eul mi-ri ye-yak-haet-seo-yo.

I reserved the ticket in advance.

Similar Expressions

• 항공편 (hang-gong-pyeon) flight (scheduled service)
• 항공기 (hang-gong-gi) aircraft (more formal)
• 비행 (bi-haeng) flight / flying (the act)
