Frequently Asked Questions - 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work])

Q1. How do Koreans say "Leave work"?

퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]) means finishing your shift and going home. Opposite of 출근([chul-geun/go to work]) and used when you say goodbye to 사장님([sa-jang-nim/boss]).

Q2. What is "Night overtime" in Korean?

야근([ya-geun/night overtime]) means staying late after normal hours. 연장근무([yeon-jang-geun-mu/overtime work]) is formal term with extra pay.

Q3. How do you say "Leaving on time" and "Way home"?

칼퇴근([kal-toe-geun/leaving exactly on time]) means leaving right at 6 o'clock, 퇴근길([toe-geun-gil/way home from work]) means commute after 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Leave Work" in Korean - 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work])

What does "퇴근([toe-geun/leave work])" mean?

퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]) means "leave work" or "get off work / clock out." It is the standard EPS-TOPIK word for finishing your shift and going home, opposite of 출근([chul-geun/go to work]).

Casual: 퇴근이야 (toe-geun-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 퇴근이에요 (toe-geun-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 퇴근입니다 (toe-geun-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]) is made of two parts — 퇴([toe/leaving]) + 근([geun/work/duty]). 야근([ya-geun/night overtime]) = 야([ya/night]) + 근([geun/work]), 칼퇴근([kal-toe-geun/leaving on time]) = 칼([kal/knife]) + 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]).

When to Use It

1. Talking about your schedule: "퇴근시간([toe-geun-si-gan/leaving time])이 언제예요? (toe-geun-si-gan-i eon-je-ye-yo)" (What time do you get off work?).
2. Explaining overtime: Being careful about 야근([ya-geun/night overtime]) and 연장근무([yeon-jang-geun-mu/overtime work]) before you 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]).
3. Daily factory life: Mentioning 퇴근길([toe-geun-gil/way home]) and using 출근카드([chul-geun-ka-deu/time card]) to record your leaving.

Real-Life Examples

매일 6시에 퇴근해요. mae-il yeo-seot-si-e toe-geun-hae-yo.

I leave work at 6 every day. - 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]) daily routine

야근 끝나고 10시에 퇴근했어요. ya-geun kkeut-na-go yeol-si-e toe-geun-hae-sseo-yo.

I left at 10 after night overtime. - 야근([ya-geun/night overtime]) + 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work])

오늘 칼퇴근하고 퇴근길에 장을 봐요. o-neul kal-toe-geun-ha-go toe-geun-gil-e jang-eul bwa-yo.

Today I leave exactly on time and shop on my way home. - 칼퇴근([kal-toe-geun/leaving on time]) + 퇴근길([toe-geun-gil/way home])
