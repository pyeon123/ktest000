Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "go to work" — 출근 (chul-geun)?

Koreans use "출근 (chul-geun)" for leaving home and arriving at the workplace — factory, office, or job site — to start the work shift. It's one of the most frequently tested EPS-TOPIK workplace words.

Q2. Is "출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-eyo)" polite enough?

Yes. "출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-eyo)" is polite and natural for talking to a manager or coworker. You can also say "출근이야 (chul-geun-i-ya)" casually with close friends.

Q3. What should I say in a formal setting?

You can say "I am going to work" — 출근합니다 (chul-geun-hamnida) — when speaking formally, for example to your employer (사장님) or during EPS-TOPIK speaking practice.

EPS TOPIK How to Say "Go to Work" in Korean

What does "출근 (chul-geun)" mean?

출근 means "go to work" or "start work." Literally "go out to duty." 출 (chul) = go out/exit, 근 (geun) = work/duty. This is a core EPS-TOPIK beginner word, since arriving at the factory or job site on time is one of the first workplace rules every foreign worker must understand for the exam and for daily life in Korea.

Casual: 출근이야 (chul-geun-i-ya) - Close friends
Polite: 출근이에요 (chul-geun-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 출근합니다 (chul-geun-ham-ni-da) - To your employer/interview

Grammar Breakdown

출 (chul) = go out, exit
근 (geun) = work, duty
→ 출근 = go out + duty → to leave for work / start your shift.

Why EPS-TOPIK Learners Need This Word

1. Understanding your work schedule: "출근 시간이 몇 시예요?" (What time do you start work?).
2. Talking with your 사장님 (boss): Reporting arrival, being on time, and asking about shift changes.
3. Related workplace words: Knowing 출근 together with 퇴근 (leave work), 월급 (salary), and 동료 (colleague) covers most beginner-level EPS-TOPIK workplace listening and reading questions.

Related Korean Sentences

출근해요. 오늘 늦으면 안 돼요. chul-geun-hae-yo. o-neul neuj-eu-myeon an dwae-yo.

I'm going to work. I can't be late today.

사장님이 출근 시간을 확인해요. sa-jang-nim-i chul-geun si-gan-eul hwa-gin-hae-yo.

The boss checks the work start time.

이번 달 월급을 받고 출근했어요. i-beon dal wol-geup-eul bat-go chul-geun-haess-eo-yo.

I received this month's salary and went to work.

Similar Expressions

• 퇴근 (toe-geun) - Leave work / finish shift
• 지각 (ji-gak) - Being late (for work or class)
• 야근 (ya-geun) - Working overtime / night shift
