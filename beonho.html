Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Account number"?

계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number]) is the number of your bank account. Companies need it to send salary.

Q2. What is "Account transfer"?

계좌이체([gye-jwa-i-che/account transfer]) means sending money from one bank account to another. It is used for salary payment.

Q3. Why is "Bank account" important in EPS-TOPIK?

은행계좌([eun-haeng-gye-jwa/bank account]) is needed to receive salary. Workers must give the correct 계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Account Number" in Korean - 계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number])

What does "계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number])" mean?

계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number]) means "account number". It is the number of a bank account. Companies need the correct 계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number]) to send salary.

Casual: 계좌번호야 (gye-jwa-beon-ho-ya)
Polite: 계좌번호예요 (gye-jwa-beon-ho-ye-yo)
Formal: 계좌번호입니다 (gye-jwa-beon-ho-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number]) = 계좌(account) + 번호(number)
은행계좌([eun-haeng-gye-jwa/bank account]) = 은행(bank) + 계좌
계좌이체([gye-jwa-i-che/account transfer]) = 계좌 + 이체(transfer)
입금계좌([ip-geum-gye-jwa/deposit account]) = 입금(deposit) + 계좌
계좌정보([gye-jwa-jeong-bo/account information]) = 계좌 + 정보(information)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Asking: “계좌번호가 뭐예요? (gye-jwa-beon-ho-ga mwo-ye-yo?)”
2. Salary: Companies use 계좌이체([gye-jwa-i-che/account transfer]) to pay salary.
3. Form: Workers must write correct 계좌정보([gye-jwa-jeong-bo/account information]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 계좌번호가 뭐예요? A: gye-jwa-beon-ho-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is the account number?

B: 123-456-789예요. B: 123-456-789-ye-yo.

B: It is 123-456-789.

A: 계좌번호 알려 주세요. A: gye-jwa-beon-ho al-lyeo ju-se-yo.

A: Please tell me the account number.

B: 네, 알려 드릴게요. B: ne, al-lyeo deu-ril-ge-yo.

B: Yes, I will tell you. - 계좌번호([gye-jwa-beon-ho/account number])

A: 은행계좌 있어요? A: eun-haeng-gye-jwa i-sseo-yo?

A: Do you have a bank account?

B: 네, 있어요. B: ne, i-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I have.

A: 은행계좌 만들었어요? A: eun-haeng-gye-jwa man-deul-eo-sseo-yo?

A: Did you open a bank account?

B: 네, 만들었어요. B: ne, man-deul-eo-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I did. - 은행계좌([eun-haeng-gye-jwa/bank account])

A: 계좌이체 됐어요? A: gye-jwa-i-che dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Was the account transfer done?

B: 네, 됐어요. B: ne, dwae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, it was done.

A: 계좌이체로 보내 주세요. A: gye-jwa-i-che-ro bo-nae ju-se-yo.

A: Please send it by account transfer.

B: 네, 알겠습니다. B: ne, al-ge-sseum-ni-da.

B: Yes, I understand. - 계좌이체([gye-jwa-i-che/account transfer])

A: 입금계좌가 뭐예요? A: ip-geum-gye-jwa-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is the deposit account?

B: 이 계좌예요. B: i gye-jwa-ye-yo.

B: It is this account.

A: 입금계좌 확인했어요? A: ip-geum-gye-jwa hwak-in-hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you check the deposit account?

B: 네, 확인했어요. B: ne, hwak-in-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I checked. - 입금계좌([ip-geum-gye-jwa/deposit account])
