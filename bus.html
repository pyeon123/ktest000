Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Bus"?

버스 (beo-seu) bus is the standard word for any city or intercity bus.

Q2. What is "Bus stop" in Korean?

정류장 (jeong-ryu-jang) bus stop is the place where buses pick up and drop off passengers.

Q3. How do you say "Transit card" and "Transfer"?

교통카드 (gyo-tong-ka-deu) transit card and 환승 (hwan-seung) transfer are essential for daily commuting.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Bus" in Korean

What does "버스 (beo-seu)" mean?

버스 (beo-seu) means "bus." It is the standard word for any city or intercity bus.

Casual: 버스야 (beo-seu-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 버스예요 (beo-seu-ye-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 버스입니다 (beo-seu-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

버스 (beo-seu) is a loanword from English “bus”. It is used for all types of buses in Korea.

When to Use It

1. Talking about transportation: "버스 타고 가요 (beo-seu ta-go ga-yo)" (I go by bus).
2. Places: Using 정류장 (jeong-ryu-jang) bus stop.
3. Daily commuting: Mentioning 하차벨 (ha-cha-bel) stop button, 교통카드 (gyo-tong-ka-deu) transit card, or 환승 (hwan-seung) transfer.

Real-Life Examples

버스가 언제 와요? beo-seu-ga eon-je wa-yo?

When does the bus come?

다음 정류장에서 내려 주세요. da-eum jeong-ryu-jang-e-seo nae-ryeo ju-se-yo.

Please let me get off at the next stop.

교통카드 찍고 타세요. gyo-tong-ka-deu jjik-go ta-se-yo.

Please tap your transit card and get on.

Similar Expressions

• 하차벨 (ha-cha-bel) stop button
• 교통카드 (gyo-tong-ka-deu) transit card
• 환승 (hwan-seung) transfer
