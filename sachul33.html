Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Injection molding"?

사출([sa-chul/injection molding]) means making plastic products by injecting melted plastic into a mold.

Q2. What is "Injection machine" and "Injection mold"?

사출기([sa-chul-gi/injection machine]) is the machine that does the injection, and 사출금형([sa-chul-geum-hyeong/injection mold]) is the metal mold used.

Q3. Why is "Injection molding process" important in EPS-TOPIK?

사출성형([sa-chul-seong-hyeong/injection molding process]) is a core factory process for making plastic parts, so it is frequently tested in EPS-TOPIK.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Injection Molding" in Korean - 사출([sa-chul/injection molding])

What does "사출([sa-chul/injection molding])" mean?

사출([sa-chul/injection molding]) means "injection molding". It is the process of making plastic products by injecting melted plastic into a mold.

Casual: 사출이야 (sa-chul-i-ya)
Polite: 사출이에요 (sa-chul-i-e-yo)
Formal: 사출입니다 (sa-chul-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

사출([sa-chul/injection molding]) = the core word for injection molding.
사출기([sa-chul-gi/injection machine]) = 사출 + 기 (machine)
사출작업([sa-chul-jak-eop/injection work]) = 사출 + 작업 (work)
사출금형([sa-chul-geum-hyeong/injection mold]) = 사출 + 금형 (mold)
사출성형([sa-chul-seong-hyeong/injection molding process]) = 사출 + 성형 (molding)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Daily work: “사출 했어요? (sa-chul hae-sseo-yo?)”
2. Machine: Check the 사출기([sa-chul-gi/injection machine]) before starting.
3. Process: 사출성형([sa-chul-seong-hyeong/injection molding process]) is a main production method.
