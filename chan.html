Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Warehouse"?

창고([chang-go/warehouse]) means warehouse or storage where factory keeps raw materials, parts, finished products with forklift and pallets for inventory management.

Q2. What is incoming and outgoing?

입고([ip-go/incoming]) is goods coming into warehouse, 출고([chul-go/outgoing]) is goods going out for shipment to customer.

Q3. What is inventory?

재고([jae-go/inventory]) means inventory or stock count currently kept in warehouse like 100개([baek-gae/100 pieces]) must be checked daily.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Warehouse" in Korean - 창고([chang-go/warehouse])

What does "창고([chang-go/warehouse])" mean?

창고([chang-go/warehouse]) means "warehouse" or "storage / place to store goods".

Casual: 창고야 (chang-go-ya)
Polite: 창고예요 (chang-go-ye-yo)
Formal: 창고입니다 (chang-go-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

창고([chang-go/warehouse]) = 창고([chang-go/warehouse]) itself. 창고관리([chang-go-gwan-ri/management]) = 창고 + 관리([gwan-ri/management]), 입고([ip-go/incoming]) = 입([ip/entering]) + 고([go/warehouse]), 출고([chul-go/outgoing]) = 출([chul/exiting]) + 고([go/warehouse]), 재고([jae-go/inventory]) = 재([jae/existing]) + 고([go/warehouse]).

When to Use It

1. Warehouse: "창고가 뭐예요? (chang-go-ga mwo-ye-yo?)"
2. In/out: 입고([ip-go/incoming]) is coming into 창고([chang-go/warehouse]), 출고([chul-go/outgoing]) is going out for 납품([nap-pum/delivery]) to 거래처([geo-rae-cheo/client]).
3. Inventory: 재고([jae-go/inventory]) is 남아있는([na-ma-it-neun/remaining]) goods like 100개([baek-gae/100 pieces]), 창고관리([chang-go-gwan-ri/warehouse management]) is management with 지게차([ji-ge-cha/forklift]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 창고가 뭐예요? A: chang-go-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is warehouse?

B: 물건 보관하는 곳이에요. B: mul-geon bo-gwan-ha-neun go-si-e-yo.

B: Place to store goods.

A: 창고 어디예요? A: chang-go eo-di-ye-yo?

A: Where is warehouse?

B: 저기예요. B: jeo-gi-ye-yo.

B: Over there. - 창고([chang-go/warehouse])

A: 입고가 뭐예요? A: ip-go-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is incoming?

B: 들어오는 거예요. B: deu-reo-o-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: Coming in.

A: 입고됐어요? A: ip-go-dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Is it warehoused?

B: 네, 입고됐어요. B: ne, ip-go-dwae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, warehoused. - 입고([ip-go/incoming])

A: 출고가 뭐예요? A: chul-go-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is outgoing?

B: 나가는 거예요. B: na-ga-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: Going out.

A: 출고됐어요? A: chul-go-dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Is it shipped out?

B: 네, 출고됐어요. B: ne, chul-go-dwae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, shipped. - 출고([chul-go/outgoing])

A: 재고가 뭐예요? A: jae-go-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is inventory?

B: 남아있는 물건이에요. B: na-ma-it-neun mul-geon-i-e-yo.

B: Remaining goods.

A: 재고 얼마예요? A: jae-go eol-ma-ye-yo?

A: How much inventory?

B: 100개예요. B: baek-gae-ye-yo.

B: 100 pieces. - 재고([jae-go/inventory])
