Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Homework"?

숙제 (suk-je) homework is the standard word for school assignments done at home.

Q2. What is "School" in Korean?

학교 (hak-gyo) school is the common word for the place where students learn.

Q3. How do you say "Teacher"?

선생님 (seon-saeng-nim) teacher is the respectful word used for educators.

EPS TOPIK: How to Say "Homework" in Korean

What does "숙제 (suk-je)" mean?

숙제 (suk-je) means "homework" – the school assignments students complete at home.

Casual: 숙제야 (suk-je-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 숙제예요 (suk-je-ye-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 숙제입니다 (suk-je-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

숙 (suk) = stay overnight / lodging
제 (je) = task / assignment
→ 숙제 (suk-je) = homework.

When to Use It

1. Checking progress: "숙제 했어? (suk-je haet-seo?)" (Did you do your homework?).
2. Going to learn: Talking about 학교 (hak-gyo) school.
3. Respectful address: Speaking to 선생님 (seon-saeng-nim) teacher.

Real-Life Examples

숙제 했어? suk-je haet-seo?

Did you do your homework?

학교 언제 가? hak-gyo eon-je ga?

When do you go to school?

선생님 어디 계세요? seon-saeng-nim eo-di gye-se-yo?

Where is the teacher?

Similar Expressions

• 학교 (hak-gyo) school
• 선생님 (seon-saeng-nim) teacher
• 숙제하다 (suk-je-ha-da) do homework
