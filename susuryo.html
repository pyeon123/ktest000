Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Fee"?

수수료([su-su-ryo/fee]) means fee you pay for bank transfer, remittance, ATM, many services in Korea.

Q2. What is exemption and discount?

수수료면제([su-su-ryo-myeon-je/fee exemption / free of charge]) is no fee, 수수료할인([su-su-ryo-hal-in/fee discount]) is discounted fee.

Q3. What is checking and payment?

수수료확인([su-su-ryo-hwa-gin/checking fee / fee verification]) is checking fee, 수수료납부([su-su-ryo-nap-bu/payment of fee]) is paying fee.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Fee" in Korean - 수수료([su-su-ryo/fee])

What does "수수료([su-su-ryo/fee])" mean?

수수료([su-su-ryo/fee]) means "fee" or "charge for service".

Casual: 수수료야 (su-su-ryo-ya)
Polite: 수수료예요 (su-su-ryo-ye-yo)
Formal: 수수료입니다 (su-su-ryo-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

수수료([su-su-ryo/fee]) = 수수료([su-su-ryo/fee]) itself. 수수료면제([su-su-ryo-myeon-je/fee exemption]) = 수수료 + 면제([myeon-je/exemption]), 수수료할인([su-su-ryo-hal-in/discount]) = 수수료 + 할인([hal-in/discount]), 수수료확인([su-su-ryo-hwa-gin/checking]) = 수수료 + 확인([hwa-gin/checking]), 수수료납부([su-su-ryo-nap-bu/payment]) = 수수료 + 납부([nap-bu/payment]).

When to Use It

1. Fee: "수수료가 뭐예요? (su-su-ryo-ga mwo-ye-yo?)"
2. Exemption/discount: 수수료면제([su-su-ryo-myeon-je/fee exemption / free of charge]) is no fee, 수수료할인([su-su-ryo-hal-in/fee discount]) is discounted fee cheaper.
3. Checking/payment: 수수료확인([su-su-ryo-hwa-gin/checking fee / fee verification]) is checking fee before transfer, 수수료납부([su-su-ryo-nap-bu/payment of fee]) is paying fee at 은행([eun-haeng/bank]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 수수료가 뭐예요? A: su-su-ryo-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is fee?

B: 서비스 요금이에요. B: seo-bi-seu yo-geum-i-e-yo.

B: It's service charge.

A: 수수료 있어요? A: su-su-ryo i-sseo-yo?

A: Is there fee?

B: 네, 1,000원이에요. B: ne, cheon-won-i-e-yo.

B: Yes, 1,000 won. - 수수료([su-su-ryo/fee])

A: 수수료면제가 뭐예요? A: su-su-ryo-myeon-je-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What is fee exemption?

B: 수수료 없는 거예요. B: su-su-ryo eom-neun geo-ye-yo.

B: It's no fee.

A: 수수료면제 돼요? A: su-su-ryo-myeon-je dwae-yo?

A: Is fee waived?

B: 네, 이번 달은 면제예요. B: ne, i-beon dal-eun myeon-je-ye-yo.

B: Yes, free this month. - 수수료면제([su-su-ryo-myeon-je/fee exemption / free of charge])

A: 수수료할인 있어요? A: su-su-ryo-hal-in i-sseo-yo?

A: Is there fee discount?

B: 네, 50% 할인이에요. B: ne, o-sip-peo-sen-teu hal-in-i-e-yo.

B: Yes, 50% discount.

A: 수수료할인 어떻게 받아요? A: su-su-ryo-hal-in eo-tteo-ke bad-a-yo?

A: How to get discount?

B: 앱에서 하면 할인돼요. B: aep-e-seo ha-myeon hal-in-dwae-yo.

B: Discounted in app. - 수수료할인([su-su-ryo-hal-in/fee discount])

A: 수수료확인 했어요? A: su-su-ryo-hwa-gin hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you check fee?

B: 네, 확인했어요. B: ne, hwa-gin-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, checked.

A: 수수료확인 어디서 해요? A: su-su-ryo-hwa-gin eo-di-seo hae-yo?

A: Where to check fee?

B: 앱에서 확인해요. B: aep-e-seo hwa-gin-hae-yo.

B: Check in app. - 수수료확인([su-su-ryo-hwa-gin/checking fee / fee verification])
