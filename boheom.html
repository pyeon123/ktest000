Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "National health insurance"?

국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance]) is national health insurance for all people in Korea, also for foreign workers, short form 건보([geon-bo/health insurance short]).

Q2. What is "NHIS corporation" and "Enrollment"?

국민건강보험공단([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-gong-dan/NHIS corporation]) is corporation managing health insurance, 국민건강보험가입([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ga-ip/enrollment]) is enrollment.

Q3. What is premium and card?

국민건강보험료([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo/national health insurance premium]) is monthly premium, 국민건강보험증([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-jeung/national health insurance card]) is insurance card or registration info.

What does "국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance])" mean?

국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance]) means "national health insurance" or "health insurance for all nationals including foreigners". Covers hospital cost.

Casual: 국민건강보험이야 (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-i-ya)
Polite: 국민건강보험이에요 (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-i-e-yo)
Formal: 국민건강보험입니다 (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance]) = 국민([guk-min/national]) + 건강([geon-gang/health]) + 보험([bo-heom/insurance]). 국민건강보험공단([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-gong-dan/NHIS corporation]) = 국민건강보험 + 공단([gong-dan/corporation]), 국민건강보험가입([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ga-ip/enrollment]) = 국민건강보험 + 가입([ga-ip/enrollment]), 국민건강보험료([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo/premium]) = 국민건강보험 + 료([ryo/premium]), 국민건강보험증([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-jeung/card]) = 국민건강보험 + 증([jeung/card]).

When to Use It

1. National health insurance: "국민건강보험이 뭐예요? (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-i mwo-ye-yo?)"
2. Corporation/enrollment: 국민건강보험공단([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-gong-dan/NHIS corporation]) is corporation in 원주([won-ju/Wonju]) managing insurance, 국민건강보험가입([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ga-ip/enrollment in national health insurance]) is employer enrolling workers within 14일([sip-sa-il/14 days]).
3. Premium/card: 국민건강보험료([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo/national health insurance premium]) is monthly premium about 3.5% worker and 3.5% employer, 국민건강보험증([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-jeung/national health insurance card]) is qualification checked by 외국인등록증([oe-gu-gin-deung-rok-jeung/ARC]).

B: 원주에 본사가 있어요. B: won-ju-e bon-sa-ga i-sseo-yo.

A: 국민건강보험료가 뭐예요? A: guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: 국민건강보험료 얼마예요? A: guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo eol-ma-ye-yo?

A: 국민건강보험증 있어요? A: guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-jeung i-sseo-yo?
