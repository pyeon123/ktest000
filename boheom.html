Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "National Health Insurance"?

국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance]) is health insurance all Koreans and registered foreigners must join, pays part of hospital costs.

Q2. What is health insurance premium?

건강보험료([geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo/health insurance premium]) is monthly fee for health insurance, employer pays half and worker pays half for workplace subscribers.

Q3. What are health insurance benefits?

건강보험혜택([geon-gang-bo-heom-hye-taek/health insurance benefits]) includes cheap hospital visit, medicine, surgery, health check, worker pays only small part.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "National Health Insurance" in Korean - 국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance])

What does "국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance])" mean?

국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance]) means "national health insurance". It is mandatory health insurance for all workers including EPS, pays part of hospital costs.

Casual: 국민건강보험이야 (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-i-ya)
Polite: 국민건강보험이에요 (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-i-e-yo)
Formal: 국민건강보험입니다 (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance]) = 국민 + 건강 + 보험 (national + health + insurance)
건강보험증([geon-gang-bo-heom-jeung/health insurance card]) = 건강보험 + 증 (health insurance + card)
건강보험료([geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo/health insurance premium]) = 건강보험 + 료 (health insurance + fee)
건강보험가입([geon-gang-bo-heom-ga-ip/health insurance enrollment]) = 건강보험 + 가입 (health insurance + enrollment)
건강보험혜택([geon-gang-bo-heom-hye-taek/health insurance benefits]) = 건강보험 + 혜택 (health insurance + benefits)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Insurance: “국민건강보험 가입됐어요? (guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom ga-ip-dwae-sseo-yo?)”
2. Premium: 건강보험료([geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo/health insurance premium]) about 7% of wage, half employer half worker.
3. Benefits: 건강보험혜택([geon-gang-bo-heom-hye-taek/health insurance benefits]) cheap hospital and medicine.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 국민건강보험 가입됐어요? A: guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom ga-ip-dwae-sseo-yo?

A: Are you enrolled in national health insurance?

B: 네, 가입됐어요. B: ne, ga-ip-dwae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, enrolled.

A: 국민건강보험으로 병원 갔어요? A: guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom-eu-ro byeong-won ga-sseo-yo?

A: Did you go to hospital with national health insurance?

B: 네, 갔어요. B: ne, ga-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, went. - 국민건강보험([guk-min-geon-gang-bo-heom/national health insurance])

A: 건강보험료 얼마예요? A: geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo eol-ma-ye-yo?

A: How much is health insurance premium?

B: 10만원이에요. B: 10-man-won-i-e-yo.

B: 100,000 won.

A: 건강보험료 누가 내요? A: geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo nu-ga nae-yo?

A: Who pays health insurance premium?

B: 회사랑 제가 반반 내요. B: hoe-sa-rang je-ga ban-ban nae-yo.

B: Company and I pay half each. - 건강보험료([geon-gang-bo-heom-ryo/health insurance premium])

A: 건강보험혜택 받았어요? A: geon-gang-bo-heom-hye-taek ba-da-sseo-yo?

A: Did you get health insurance benefits?

B: 네, 받았어요. B: ne, ba-da-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, got benefits.

A: 건강보험혜택이 뭐예요? A: geon-gang-bo-heom-hye-taek-i mwo-ye-yo?

A: What are health insurance benefits?

B: 병원을 싸게 갈 수 있어요. B: byeong-won-eul ssa-ge gal su i-sseo-yo.

B: Can go to hospital cheaply. - 건강보험혜택([geon-gang-bo-heom-hye-taek/health insurance benefits])
