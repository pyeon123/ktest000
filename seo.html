Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Application form"?

신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form]) is the paper you fill out when you want to apply for something, such as a job, visa, or leave.

Q2. What is "Filling out"?

작성([jak-seong/filling out]) means writing information on the form. Always fill out the 신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form]) carefully.

Q3. Why is "Submission" important in EPS-TOPIK?

제출([je-chul/submission]) means giving the completed form to the office. After 작성([jak-seong/filling out]), you must do 제출([je-chul/submission]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Application Form" in Korean - 신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form])

What does "신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form])" mean?

신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form]) means "application form". It is the paper you fill out when you want to apply for leave, a job, a visa, or other requests.

Casual: 신청서야 (sin-cheong-seo-ya)
Polite: 신청서예요 (sin-cheong-seo-ye-yo)
Formal: 신청서입니다 (sin-cheong-seo-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form]) = 신청(application) + 서(document)
작성([jak-seong/filling out]) = writing information on the form
제출([je-chul/submission]) = giving the completed form
신청([sin-cheong/application]) = the act of applying
구비서류([gu-bi-seo-ryu/required documents]) = necessary papers

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Asking: “신청서 있어요? (sin-cheong-seo i-sseo-yo?)”
2. Action: “신청서 작성하세요. (sin-cheong-seo jak-seong-ha-se-yo.)”
3. Final step: “제출하세요. (je-chul-ha-se-yo.)”

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 신청서 있어요? A: sin-cheong-seo i-sseo-yo?

A: Do you have the application form?

B: 네, 있어요. B: ne, i-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I have.

A: 신청서 작성하세요. A: sin-cheong-seo jak-seong-ha-se-yo.

A: Please fill out the application form.

B: 네, 할게요. B: ne, 할-ge-yo.

B: Yes, I will. - 신청서([sin-cheong-seo/application form])

A: 작성했어요? A: jak-seong-hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you fill it out?

B: 네, 했어요. B: ne, hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I did.

A: 작성해 주세요. A: jak-seong-hae ju-se-yo.

A: Please fill it out.

B: 네, 알겠습니다. B: ne, al-ge-sseum-ni-da.

B: Yes, I understand. - 작성([jak-seong/filling out])

A: 제출했어요? A: je-chul-hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you submit it?

B: 네, 했어요. B: ne, hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I did.

A: 어디에 제출해요? A: eo-di-e je-chul-hae-yo?

A: Where do I submit it?

B: 사무실에 제출하세요. B: sa-mu-sil-e je-chul-ha-se-yo.

B: Please submit it to the office. - 제출([je-chul/submission])

A: 구비서류가 뭐예요? A: gu-bi-seo-ryu-ga mwo-ye-yo?

A: What are the required documents?

B: 신분증하고 사진이에요. B: sin-bun-jeung-ha-go sa-jin-i-e-yo.

B: ID card and photo.

A: 구비서류 준비했어요? A: gu-bi-seo-ryu jun-bi-hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you prepare the required documents?

B: 네, 준비했어요. B: ne, jun-bi-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I prepared them. - 구비서류([gu-bi-seo-ryu/required documents])
