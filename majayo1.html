Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "That's right" — 맞아요 (majayo)?

Koreans use it to agree with what someone said.

Q2. Is "That's right" — 맞아요 (majayo) polite enough?

Yes. "맞아요 (majayo)" is polite and natural. You can also say "맞아 (maja)" casually.

Q3. What should I say when I agree with a friend?

You can say "That's right, exactly" — 맞아요, 정확해요 (majayo, jeonghwakhaeyo).
