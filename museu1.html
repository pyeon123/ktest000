Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "What happened?" — 무슨 일이에요? (museun irieyo?)

Koreans use it when they see someone upset or notice something unusual.

Q2. Is "What happened?" — 무슨 일이에요? (museun irieyo?) polite enough?

Yes. "무슨 일이에요? (museun irieyo?)" is polite and caring. You can also say "무슨 일?" casually with friends.

Q3. What should I say when a friend looks sad?

You can say "What happened? Are you okay?" — 무슨 일이에요? 괜찮아요? (museun irieyo? gwaenchanayo?).

Learn More: How to Say "What Happened?" in Korean

What does "무슨 일이에요? (museun irieyo)" mean?

무슨 일이에요? means "What happened? / What's the matter?" Caring expression. 무슨 (museun) = what kind of, 일 (il) = matter/affair, 이에요? (ieyo?) = is it? Polite question. Used when someone looks upset or something unusual happens.

Polite: 무슨 일이에요? (mu-seun il-i-e-yo?) - Standard caring
Formal: 무슨 일입니까? (mu-seun il-im-ni-kka?) - Elders, formal
Casual: 무슨 일? (mu-seun il?) - Close friends

Grammar Breakdown

무슨 (museun) = what, what kind of
일 (il) = work, matter, event
이에요? / 입니까? (ieyo? / imnikka?) = is it? (question)
→ 무슨 일이에요? = What matter is it? → What happened?

When to Use It

1. Friend looks sad: Show concern.
2. Something unusual: Ask what's wrong.
3. Empathy: Build closer relationship.

Real-Life Examples

무슨 일이에요? 괜찮아요? mu-seun il-i-e-yo? gwaen-chan-a-yo?

What happened? Are you okay?

무슨 일입니까? 도움이 필요하세요? mu-seun il-im-ni-kka? do-um-i pil-yo-ha-se-yo?

What happened? Do you need help? (Formal)

무슨 일? 말해봐. mu-seun il? mal-hae-bwa.

What happened? Tell me. (Casual)

별일 아니에요. byeol-il an-i-e-yo.

It's nothing. (Reply)

Similar Expressions

• 왜 그래요? (wae geuraeyo?) - What's wrong?
• 괜찮아요? (gwaenchanayo?) - Are you okay?
• 무슨 일이야? (museun iriya?) - What happened? (Casual)
