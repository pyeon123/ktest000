Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "Water please" — 물 주세요 (mul juseyo)?

Koreans use it at restaurants, cafés, or when they need water while eating or drinking.

Q2. Is "Water please" — 물 주세요 (mul juseyo) polite enough for restaurants?

Yes. "물 주세요 (mul juseyo)" is polite and commonly used. You can also say "물 좀 주세요 (mul jom juseyo)" for a softer request.

Q3. What should I say if I want more water?

You can say "More water please" — 물 더 주세요 (mul deo juseyo) or "Water refill please" — 물 리필 해주세요 (mul ri-pil hae juseyo).

Learn More: How to Say "Water, Please" in Korean

What does "물 주세요 (mul juseyo)" mean?

물 주세요 means "Water please" / "Please give me water." 물 (mul) = water, 주세요 (juseyo) = please give. Most useful restaurant phrase in Korea. Water is usually free, but asking politely shows good manners.

Polite: 물 주세요 (mul ju-se-yo) - Standard polite
Formal: 물을 주십시오 (mu-reul ju-sip-si-o) - Upscale, formal
Casual: 물 줘 (mul jwo) / 물 좀 (mul jom) - Friends, casual

Grammar Breakdown

물 (mul) = water (noun)
주다 (juda) = to give
주세요 (juseyo) = please give / polite request
→ 물 주세요 = Please give water → Water please.

When to Use It

1. Restaurant/cafe: Need water while eating.
2. Spicy/salty food: Need drink.
3. Home/office: Ask for water politely.

Real-Life Examples

물 주세요. 감사합니다. mul ju-se-yo. gam-sa-ham-ni-da.

Water please. Thank you.

물 좀 주세요. 매워요. mul jom ju-se-yo. mae-wo-yo.

Some water please. It's spicy.

물 더 주세요. mul deo ju-se-yo.

More water please.

시원한 물 주세요. si-won-han mul ju-se-yo.

Cold water please.

Similar Expressions

• 물 더 주세요 (mul deo juseyo) - More water please
• 물 리필 해주세요 (mul ripil haejuseyo) - Water refill please
• 따뜻한 물 주세요 (ttatteuthan mul juseyo) - Warm water please
