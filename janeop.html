Frequently Asked Questions - 잔업([jan-eop/extra work])

Q1. How do Koreans say "Remaining work"?

잔업([jan-eop/extra work]) means tasks left after normal hours. It is extra work you finish before 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]).

Q2. What is "Additional work" and "Overtime"?

추가근무([chu-ga-geun-mu/additional work]) is extra work added, 연장근무([yeon-jang-geun-mu/overtime]) and 초과근무([cho-gwa-geun-mu/excess work]) mean extended hours beyond normal time.

Q3. How do you say "Extra pay"?

잔업수당([jan-eop-su-dang/extra work pay]) is extra money you get for 잔업([jan-eop/extra work]) and 야근([ya-geun/night overtime]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Remaining Work" in Korean - 잔업([jan-eop/extra work])

What does "잔업([jan-eop/extra work])" mean?

잔업([jan-eop/extra work]) means "remaining work" or "extra work after hours". It is the standard EPS-TOPIK word for tasks left after normal 퇴근시간([toe-geun-si-gan/leaving time]).

Casual: 잔업이야 (jan-eob-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 잔업이에요 (jan-eob-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 잔업입니다 (jan-eob-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

잔업([jan-eop/extra work]) = 잔([jan/remaining]) + 업([eop/work]). 추가근무([chu-ga-geun-mu/additional work]) = 추가([chu-ga/adding]) + 근무([geun-mu/duty]), 잔업수당([jan-eop-su-dang/extra work pay]) = 잔업([jan-eop/extra work]) + 수당([su-dang/allowance]).

When to Use It

1. Talking about extra tasks: "오늘 잔업([jan-eop/extra work]) 있어요? (o-neul jan-eop i-sseo-yo?)"
2. Explaining overtime: Mentioning 연장근무([yeon-jang-geun-mu/overtime]) and 초과근무([cho-gwa-geun-mu/excess work]) before 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]).
3. Checking pay: Asking about 잔업수당([jan-eop-su-dang/extra work pay]) on 급여명세서([geu-yeo-myeong-se-seo/payslip]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B Dialogue

A: 오늘 잔업 있어요? B: 네, 잔업 2시간 있어요. A: o-neul jan-eop i-sseo-yo? B: ne, jan-eop du si-gan i-sseo-yo.

Is there extra work today? Yes, 2 hours extra.

A: 초과근무 끝나고 몇 시에 퇴근해요? B: 9시에 퇴근해요. A: cho-gwa-geun-mu kkeut-na-go myeot si-e toe-geun-hae-yo? B: a-hop-si-e toe-geun-hae-yo.

What time do you leave after excess work? I leave at 9.

A: 잔업수당 얼마예요? B: 한 시간에 만 원이에요. A: jan-eop-su-dang eol-ma-ye-yo? B: han si-gan-e man won-i-e-yo.

How much is extra work pay? It's 10,000 won per hour.
