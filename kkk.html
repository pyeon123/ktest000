Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Fire Shutter" in factories?

방화셔터([bang-hwa-syeo-teo/fire shutter]) means a special door that closes automatically to stop fire from spreading. It is a core EPS-TOPIK safety word.

Q2. What is the difference between 방화셔터 and 방화셔터작동?

방화셔터([bang-hwa-syeo-teo/fire shutter]) is the equipment itself. 방화셔터작동([bang-hwa-syeo-teo-jak-dong/fire shutter operation]) means the shutter is working or moving.

Q3. Why is 방화셔터점검 important?

방화셔터점검([bang-hwa-syeo-teo-jeom-geom/fire shutter inspection]) is the regular check to make sure the shutter works correctly. It prevents fire from spreading and keeps workers safe.

What does "방화셔터([bang-hwa-syeo-teo/fire shutter])" mean?

방화셔터([bang-hwa-syeo-teo/fire shutter]) means "fire shutter". It is a special door that closes automatically to stop fire from spreading.

Casual: 방화셔터야 (bang-hwa-syeo-teo-ya)
Polite: 방화셔터예요 (bang-hwa-syeo-teo-ye-yo)
Formal: 방화셔터입니다 (bang-hwa-syeo-teo-im-ni-da)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Asking about check: “오늘 방화셔터 점검해요?”
2. Operation: “방화셔터작동 확인했어요?”
3. Inspection: “방화셔터점검 했어요?”
