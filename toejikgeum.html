Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Severance pay"?

퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]) means money given when leaving job after working 1 year or more.

Q2. What is "Severance pay settlement" and "calculation"?

퇴직금정산([toe-jik-geum-jeong-san/severance pay settlement]) is settling severance pay, 퇴직금계산([toe-jik-geum-gye-san/severance pay calculation]) is calculating how much: average wage x 30 days x years.

Q3. What is receiving severance pay?

퇴직금수령([toe-jik-geum-su-ryeong/receiving severance pay]) means receiving 퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]), 퇴직금제도([toe-jik-geum-je-do/severance pay system]) is system that guarantees it.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Severance pay" in Korean - 퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay])

What does "퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay])" mean?

퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]) means "severance pay" or "money given when leaving job after 1 year". Big money when you quit after long work.

Casual: 퇴직금이야 (toe-jik-geum-i-ya)
Polite: 퇴직금이에요 (toe-jik-geum-i-e-yo)
Formal: 퇴직금입니다 (toe-jik-geum-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay]) = 퇴직([toe-jik/retirement]) + 금([geum/money]). 퇴직금정산([toe-jik-geum-jeong-san/severance settlement]) = 퇴직금 + 정산([jeong-san/settlement]), 퇴직금계산([toe-jik-geum-gye-san/calculation]) = 퇴직금 + 계산([gye-san/calculation]), 퇴직금수령([toe-jik-geum-su-ryeong/receiving]) = 퇴직금 + 수령([su-ryeong/receiving]), 퇴직금제도([toe-jik-geum-je-do/system]) = 퇴직금 + 제도([je-do/system]).

When to Use It

1. Severance pay: "퇴직금 받아요? (toe-jik-geum bad-a-yo?)"
2. Calculation/settlement: 퇴직금계산([toe-jik-geum-gye-san/severance pay calculation]) = 평균임금([pyeong-gyun-im-geum/average wage]) x 30일([sam-sip-il/30 days]) x years, 퇴직금정산([toe-jik-geum-jeong-san/settlement]) within 14일([sip-sa-il/14 days]).
3. System: 퇴직금수령([toe-jik-geum-su-ryeong/receiving severance pay]) is receiving, 퇴직금제도([toe-jik-geum-je-do/severance pay system]) is law guaranteeing it after 1년([il-lyeon/1 year]).

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 퇴직금 받아요? A: toe-jik-geum bad-a-yo?

A: Do you get severance pay?

B: 네, 1년 넘으면 받아요. B: ne, il-lyeon neom-eu-myeon bad-a-yo.

B: Yes, if over 1 year I get.

A: 퇴직금 얼마예요? A: toe-jik-geum eol-ma-ye-yo?

A: How much is severance pay?

B: 300만원이에요. B: sam-baek-man-won-i-e-yo.

B: It's 3 million won. - 퇴직금([toe-jik-geum/severance pay])

A: 퇴직금정산 받았어요? A: toe-jik-geum-jeong-san bad-a-sseo-yo?

A: Did you get severance pay settlement?

B: 네, 받았어요. B: ne, bad-a-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I got.

A: 퇴직금정산 언제 해요? A: toe-jik-geum-jeong-san eon-je hae-yo?

A: When is severance pay settlement?

B: 퇴직 후 14일 안에 해요. B: toe-jik hu sip-sa-il an-e hae-yo.

B: Within 14 days after retirement. - 퇴직금정산([toe-jik-geum-jeong-san/severance pay settlement])

A: 퇴직금계산 어떻게 해요? A: toe-jik-geum-gye-san eo-tteo-ke hae-yo?

A: How to calculate severance pay?

B: 평균임금 곱하기 30일이에요. B: pyeong-gyun-im-geum gop-ha-gi sam-sip-il-i-e-yo.

B: Average wage times 30 days.

A: 퇴직금계산 해주세요. A: toe-jik-geum-gye-san hae-ju-se-yo.

A: Please calculate severance pay.

B: 네, 계산해 드릴게요. B: ne, gye-san-hae deu-ril-ge-yo.

B: Yes, I'll calculate. - 퇴직금계산([toe-jik-geum-gye-san/severance pay calculation])

A: 퇴직금수령 했어요? A: toe-jik-geum-su-ryeong hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you receive severance pay?

B: 네, 통장에 들어왔어요. B: ne, tong-jang-e deul-eo-wa-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, it entered bank account.

A: 퇴직금수령 언제 해요? A: toe-jik-geum-su-ryeong eon-je hae-yo?

A: When do you receive severance pay?

B: 퇴직 후 14일 안에 받아요. B: toe-jik hu sip-sa-il an-e bad-a-yo.

B: I get within 14 days after retirement. - 퇴직금수령([toe-jik-geum-su-ryeong/receiving severance pay])
