Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Uncomfortable"?

불편하다([bul-pyeon-ha-da/uncomfortable]) means uncomfortable - not at ease, inconvenient. In polite speech, say 불편해요([bul-pyeon-hae-yo]). Example: 이 신발 불편해요 (These shoes are uncomfortable).

Q2. Difference between 불편하다 and 편하다?

불편하다 is opposite of 편하다. 편하다 is comfortable, at ease. 불편하다 is uncomfortable, inconvenient. 편한 의자 (comfortable chair) vs 불편한 의자 (uncomfortable chair). EPS-TOPIK uses both often.

Q3. Does 불편하다 have other grammar forms?

Yes. 불편했어요([bul-pyeon-hae-sseo-yo/was uncomfortable]) is past, 너무 불편해요([neo-mu bul-pyeon-hae-yo/so uncomfortable]) so uncomfortable, 불편하지 않아요([bul-pyeon-ha-ji a-na-yo/isn't uncomfortable]) negative, and 편하게 해 주세요([pyeon-ha-ge hae ju-se-yo/please make it comfortable]) means please make it comfortable.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Uncomfortable" in Korean - 불편하다([bul-pyeon-ha-da/uncomfortable])

What does "불편하다([bul-pyeon-ha-da/uncomfortable])" mean?

불편하다([bul-pyeon-ha-da/uncomfortable]) means "uncomfortable". Not at ease, awkward, inconvenient, hard to stay.

Casual: 불편해 (bul-pyeon-hae)
Polite: 불편해요 (bul-pyeon-hae-yo)
Formal: 불편합니다 (bul-pyeon-ham-ni-da)
Before a noun: 불편한 (bul-pyeon-han) - 불편한 자리 (uncomfortable seat)

Grammar Breakdown

불편하다([bul-pyeon-ha-da/uncomfortable]) = 불편 (inconvenience) + 하다
불편했어요([bul-pyeon-hae-sseo-yo/was uncomfortable]) = 불편했어요
너무 불편해요([neo-mu bul-pyeon-hae-yo/so uncomfortable]) = 너무 + 불편해요
불편하지 않아요([bul-pyeon-ha-ji a-na-yo/isn't uncomfortable]) = 불편하 + 지 않아요
편하게 해 주세요([pyeon-ha-ge hae ju-se-yo/please make it comfortable]) = 편하게 + 해 주세요

When to Use It

1. Uncomfortable: “이 신발 불편해요 (i sin-bal bul-pyeon-hae-yo)”
2. Past: 불편했어요([bul-pyeon-hae-sseo-yo/was uncomfortable]) was uncomfortable.
3. Emphasis: 너무 불편해요([neo-mu bul-pyeon-hae-yo/so uncomfortable]) so uncomfortable.
4. Negative: 불편하지 않아요([bul-pyeon-ha-ji a-na-yo/isn't uncomfortable]) isn't uncomfortable.
5. Request: 편하게 해 주세요([pyeon-ha-ge hae ju-se-yo/please make it comfortable]) please make it comfortable.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 불편하다? A: bul-pyeon-ha-da?

A: Are you uncomfortable?

B: 네, 불편해요. B: ne, bul-pyeon-hae-yo.

B: Yes, I'm uncomfortable.

A: 뭐가 불편해요? A: mwo-ga bul-pyeon-hae-yo?

A: What is uncomfortable?

B: 이 신발이 불편해요. B: i sin-ba-ri bul-pyeon-hae-yo.

B: These shoes are uncomfortable. - 불편하다([bul-pyeon-ha-da/uncomfortable])

A: 괜찮아요? A: gwaen-cha-na-yo?

A: Are you okay?

B: 너무 불편해요. B: neo-mu bul-pyeon-hae-yo.

B: So uncomfortable.

A: 어디가 불편해요? A: eo-di-ga bul-pyeon-hae-yo?

A: Where uncomfortable?

B: 다리가 아파요. B: da-ri-ga a-pa-yo.

B: Leg hurts. - 너무 불편해요([neo-mu bul-pyeon-hae-yo/so uncomfortable])

A: 불편해요? A: bul-pyeon-hae-yo?

A: Uncomfortable?

B: 네, 좀요. B: ne, jom-yo.

B: Yes, a bit.

A: 편하게 해 주세요. A: pyeon-ha-ge hae ju-se-yo.

A: Please make it comfortable.

B: 네, 바꿔 드릴게요. B: ne, ba-kkwo deu-ril-ge-yo.

B: Yes, will change. - 편하게 해 주세요([pyeon-ha-ge hae ju-se-yo/please make it comfortable])
