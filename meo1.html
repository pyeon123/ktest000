Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "Let's eat" — 먹어요 (meogeoyo)?

When food is ready and you want to invite others warmly. Most heartwarming daily expression in Korea, used at family meals, restaurants, with colleagues.

Q2. What's the difference between 먹어요, 먹어, and 먹읍시다?

먹어요 (meogeoyo) = polite standard. 먹어 (meogeo) = casual with friends/family. 먹읍시다 (meogeupsida) = formal group invitation.

Q3. What do Koreans say before eating?

Say 잘 먹겠습니다 (jal meokgesseumnida) = I will eat well, before eating, and 잘 먹었습니다 (jal meogeosseumnida) = I ate well, after eating.

Learn More: How to Say "Let's Eat" in Korean

What does "먹어요 (meogeoyo)" mean?

먹어요 (meogeoyo) means "Let's eat." From verb 먹다 (meokda) = to eat. 먹어요 (meogeoyo) = polite form. Heartwarming daily expression in Korea, central to building relationships.

Polite: 먹어요 (meog-eo-yo) - Standard polite
Formal: 먹읍시다 (meog-eup-si-da) - Formal group invitation
Casual: 먹어 (meog-eo) - Friends, family

Grammar Breakdown

먹다 (meok-da) = to eat
먹어요 (meog-eo-yo) = eat (polite)
먹다 (meok-da) → 먹어요 (meog-eo-yo) = to eat → Let's eat (polite)
→ 먹어요 (meog-eo-yo) = Let's eat.

When to Use It

1. Food ready: Table is set, invite others.
2. Family/friends: Warm invitation.
3. Restaurant: Encourage everyone to enjoy.

Real-Life Examples

먹어요. 맛있게 먹겠습니다. (meogeoyo. masitge meokgetseumnida.) meog-eo-yo. mat-iss-ge meok-get-seum-ni-da.

Let's eat. I will enjoy the meal.

먹읍시다. 맛있게 드십시오. (meogeupsida. masitge deusipsio.) meog-eup-si-da. mat-iss-ge deu-sip-si-o.

Let's eat. Enjoy your meal. (Formal)

먹어. 배고파 죽겠어. 빨리 먹자! (meogeo. baegopa jukgesseo. ppalli meokja!) meog-eo. bae-go-pa juk-get-seo. ppal-li meok-ja!

Let's eat. I'm starving. Let's eat quickly! (Casual)

많이 드세요. (mani deuseyo.) man-i deu-se-yo.

Eat a lot. (Polite)

Similar Expressions

• 잘 먹겠습니다 (jal meokgesseumnida) - I will eat well (before meal)
• 잘 먹었습니다 (jal meogeosseumnida) - I ate well (after meal)
• 많이 드세요 (mani deuseyo) - Eat a lot
