Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Heavy"?

무겁다([mu-geop-da/heavy]) is the basic adjective for heavy. In polite speech, say 무거워요([mu-geo-wo-yo]). The opposite is 가볍다([ga-byeop-da]), "light". Example: 이 가방이 무거워요 (This bag is heavy).

Q2. How do I use "heavy" before a noun?

Korean adjectives change before a noun. 무겁다 becomes 무거운 (mu-geo-un), so say 무거운 가방 (a heavy bag), 무거운 짐 (heavy luggage), or 무거운 책임 (heavy responsibility). Without a noun, use 무거워요: 가방이 무거워요 (The bag is heavy).

Q3. Does 무겁다 have other grammar forms?

Yes. 무거웠어요([mu-geo-wo-sseo-yo/was heavy]) is past tense, 너무 무거워요([neo-mu mu-geo-wo-yo/too heavy]) means too heavy, 무겁지 않아요([mu-geop-ji a-na-yo/isn't heavy]) is negative. For requesting, say 더 무거운 거 주세요([deo mu-geo-un geo ju-se-yo/a heavier one, please]) - though usually you'd ask for lighter.

What does "무겁다([mu-geop-da/heavy])" mean?

무겁다([mu-geop-da/heavy]) means "heavy". It is used for heavy bags, heavy luggage, or heavy responsibility. The opposite is 가볍다([ga-byeop-da]) "light".

Casual: 무거워 (mu-geo-wo)
Polite: 무거워요 (mu-geo-wo-yo)
Formal: 무겁습니다 (mu-geop-seum-ni-da)
Before a noun: 무거운 (mu-geo-un) - 무거운 가방 (a heavy bag)

Grammar Breakdown

무겁다([mu-geop-da/heavy]) = 무겁 (stem: heavy) + 다 (dictionary ending)
무거웠어요([mu-geo-wo-sseo-yo/was heavy]) = 무겁 + 었어요 → 무거웠어요 (heavy + past)
너무 무거워요([neo-mu mu-geo-wo-yo/too heavy]) = 너무 (too) + 무거워요 (is heavy)
무겁지 않아요([mu-geop-ji a-na-yo/isn't heavy]) = 무겁 + 지 않아요 (heavy + isn't)
무거운 거([mu-geo-un geo/a heavy one]) = 무거운 (before a noun) + 거 (thing)
더 무거운 거 주세요([deo mu-geo-un geo ju-se-yo/a heavier one, please]) = 더 (more) + 무거운 거 (heavy one) + 주세요 (please give)

When to Use It

1. Heavy: “이 가방이 무거워요? (i ga-bang-i mu-geo-wo-yo?)”
2. Past: 무거웠어요([mu-geo-wo-sseo-yo/was heavy]) was heavy.
3. Too much: 너무 무거워요([neo-mu mu-geo-wo-yo/too heavy]) too heavy.
4. Negative: 무겁지 않아요([mu-geop-ji a-na-yo/isn't heavy]) isn't heavy.
5. Request: 더 무거운 거 주세요([deo mu-geo-un geo ju-se-yo/a heavier one, please]) a heavier one, please.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 이 가방이 무거워요? A: i ga-bang-i mu-geo-wo-yo?

A: Is this bag heavy?

B: 네, 아주 무거워요. B: ne, a-ju mu-geo-wo-yo.

B: Yes, it's very heavy.

A: 들 수 있어요? A: deul su i-sseo-yo?

A: Can you lift it?

B: 아니요, 너무 무거워요. B: a-ni-yo, neo-mu mu-geo-wo-yo.

B: No, it's too heavy. - 무겁다([mu-geop-da/heavy])

A: 이 짐 어때요? A: i jim eo-ttae-yo?

A: How is this luggage?

B: 너무 무거워요. B: neo-mu mu-geo-wo-yo.

B: It's too heavy.

A: 가벼운 거 드릴까요? A: ga-byeo-un geo deu-ril-kka-yo?

A: Shall I give you a lighter one?

B: 네, 주세요. B: ne, ju-se-yo.

B: Yes, please. - 너무 무거워요([neo-mu mu-geo-wo-yo/too heavy])

A: 이 아령은 가벼워요. A: i a-ryeong-eun ga-byeo-wo-yo.

A: This dumbbell is light.

B: 더 무거운 거 드릴까요? B: deo mu-geo-un geo deu-ril-kka-yo?

B: Shall I give you a heavier one?

A: 네, 더 무거운 거 주세요. A: ne, deo mu-geo-un geo ju-se-yo.

A: Yes, a heavier one, please.

B: 잠깐만 기다리세요. B: jam-kkan-man gi-da-ri-se-yo.

B: Please wait a moment. - 더 무거운 거 주세요([deo mu-geo-un geo ju-se-yo/a heavier one, please])
