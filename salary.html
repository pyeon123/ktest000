Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "salary" — 월급 (wolgeup)?

Koreans use "월급 (wolgeup)" for the fixed pay a worker receives every month from their employer — a key term in everyday work life and in the EPS-TOPIK exam.

Q2. Is "월급이에요 (wolgeup-i-eyo)" polite enough?

Yes. "월급이에요 (wolgeup-i-eyo)" is polite and natural for everyday conversation. You can also say "월급이야 (wolgeup-i-ya)" casually with close friends.

Q3. What should I say in a formal setting?

You can say "It is my salary" — 월급입니다 (wolgeup-imnida) — when speaking formally, for example to a manager or during a workplace interview.

Learn More: How to Say "Salary" in Korean

What does "월급 (wol-geup)" mean?

월급 means "salary" or "monthly pay." Literally "month pay." 월 (wol) = month, 급 (geup) = pay/wage. It refers to the fixed pay a worker receives every month from an employer — one of the most important words in EPS-TOPIK workplace vocabulary.

Casual: 월급이야 (wol-geup-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 월급이에요 (wol-geup-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 월급입니다 (wol-geup-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

월 (wol) = month
급 (geup) = pay, wage
→ 월급 = money paid every month → salary / monthly pay.

When to Use It

1. Talking about payday: "월급이 언제예요?" (When is payday?).
2. Discussing your contract: Checking the 월급 amount in your 계약서.
3. Describing workplace life: Mentioning 사장님 (boss), 잔업 (overtime), or 통장 (bank account) related to it.

Real-Life Examples

이번 달 월급을 받았어요. i-beon dal wol-geub-eul ba-da-sseo-yo.

I received this month's salary.

사장님이 월급을 통장에 넣어 주세요. sa-jang-nim-i wol-geub-eul tong-jang-e neo-eo ju-se-yo.

The boss deposits my salary into my bank account.

오늘 잔업이 있어서 월급이 많아. o-neul jan-eob-i i-sseo-seo wol-geub-i man-a.

I did overtime today, so my pay is higher. (Casual)

계약서에 월급이 정확히 적혀 있습니다. gye-yak-seo-e wol-geub-i jeong-hwa-ki jeog-hyeo iss-seum-ni-da.

The salary is stated exactly in the contract. (Formal)

Similar Expressions

• 임금 (im-geum) - Wage (formal/legal term used in labor law)
• 급여 (geu-byeo) - Pay/salary (formal term used in HR documents)
• 보너스 (bo-neo-seu) - Bonus, extra pay on top of regular salary
