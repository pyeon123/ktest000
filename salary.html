Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do workers talk about "월급 (wol-geup)" in Korean?

Foreign workers and employees in Korea use "월급 (wol-geup)" to refer to their regular monthly salary or paycheck received from factories and companies.

Q2. What is the difference between "기본급" and "수당"?

"기본급 (base pay)" is the contracted standard wage, while "수당 (allowance)" includes extra pay for overtime (연장근로), night shifts (야간근로), or holiday work (휴일근로).

Q3. What is "실수령액" on an EPS-TOPIK salary slip?

"실수령액" refers to the actual net take-home salary deposited into your bank account after all deductions (공제), such as taxes and national insurance, are subtracted from total earnings.

Learn More: How to Say "Salary" in Korean (EPS-TOPIK)

What does "월급 (wol-geup)" mean?

월급 means "monthly salary" or "paycheck." Literally "monthly pay." 월 (wol) = month, 급 (geup) = pay/salary. It refers to regular monthly wage payments made by employers in Korean factories and workplaces.

Casual: 월급이야 (wol-geup-i-ya) - Friends/co-workers informally
Polite: 월급이에요 (wol-geup-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 월급입니다 (wol-geup-im-ni-da) - Official/EPS-TOPIK exam context

Grammar Breakdown

월 (wol) = month
급 (geup) = compensation, pay
→ 월급 = regular compensation paid monthly → monthly salary / paycheck.

When to Use It in EPS-TOPIK & Workplaces

1. Asking about payday: "월급날이 언제예요?" (When is payday?).
2. Checking wage details: Reviewing 기본급 (base pay), 수당 (overtime allowance), and 공제 (deductions).
3. Bank transactions: Checking direct wage deposits in your 통장 (bankbook).

Real-Life EPS-TOPIK Examples

이번 달 월급이 통장으로 들어왔어요. i-beon dal wol-geup-i tong-jang-eu-ro deu-reo-wa-sseo-yo.

This month's salary was deposited into my bank account.

월급 명세서에서 수당과 공제 내역을 확인해요. wol-geup myeong-se-seo-e-seo su-dang-gwa gong-je nae-yeok-eul hwak-in-hae-yo.

Check the allowance and deduction details on the pay stub.

기본급과 연장 근로 수당을 합산합니다. gi-bon-geup-gwa yeon-jang geun-ro su-dang-eul hap-san-ham-ni-da.

Add basic pay and overtime allowance together.

세금과 보험료를 공제한 후 실수령액을 받습니다. se-geum-gwa bo-heom-ryo-reul gong-je-han hu sil-su-ryeong-aek-eul bat-seum-ni-da.

Receive net take-home salary after deducting taxes and insurance premiums. (Formal)
