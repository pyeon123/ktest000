Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Generator"?

발전기([bal-jeon-gi/generator]) is machine that makes electricity like makes power.

Q2. What is turn on generator?

발전기 켜다([bal-jeon-gi kyeo-da/turn on generator]) is turn on generator like start power-making machine.

Q3. What is turn off generator?

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Generator" in Korean - 발전기([bal-jeon-gi/generator])

What does "발전기([bal-jeon-gi/generator])" mean?

발전기([bal-jeon-gi/generator]) means "generator". It is machine that makes electricity like makes power.

Casual: 발전기야 (bal-jeon-gi-ya)
Polite: 발전기예요 (bal-jeon-gi-ye-yo)
Formal: 발전기입니다 (bal-jeon-gi-m-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

발전기([bal-jeon-gi/generator]) = 발전기 (generator)
발전기 켜다([bal-jeon-gi kyeo-da/turn on generator]) = 발전기 + 켜다 (generator + turn on)
발전기 끄다([bal-jeon-gi kkeu-da/turn off generator]) = 발전기 + 끄다 (generator + turn off)
발전기 고장([bal-jeon-gi go-jang/generator failure]) = 발전기 + 고장 (generator + failure)
비상 발전기([bi-sang bal-jeon-gi/emergency generator]) = 비상 + 발전기 (emergency + generator)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Generator: “발전기 어디 있어요? (bal-jeon-gi eo-di i-sseo-yo?)”
2. Turn on: 발전기 켜다([bal-jeon-gi kyeo-da/turn on generator]) turn on generator.
3. Turn off: 발전기 끄다([bal-jeon-gi kkeu-da/turn off generator]) turn off generator.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 발전기 어디 있어요? A: bal-jeon-gi eo-di i-sseo-yo?

B: In warehouse.

A: 발전기 켰어요? A: bal-jeon-gi kyeo-sseo-yo?

A: Did turn on generator?

B: 네, 켰어요. B: ne, kyeo-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, turned on. - 발전기([bal-jeon-gi/generator])

A: 발전기 켰어요? A: bal-jeon-gi kyeo-sseo-yo?
