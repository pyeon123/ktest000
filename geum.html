Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Plating"?

도금([do-geum/plating]) is coating metal surface with thin layer of gold, nickel, chrome to make shiny, rust-proof.

Q2. What is plating solution?

도금액([do-geum-aek/plating solution]) is liquid in tank containing gold, nickel, chrome chemicals for coating metal surface.

Q3. What is plating defect?

도금불량([do-geum-bul-ryang/plating defect]) is bad coating problem like peeling, spot, uneven color, no shine after coating.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Plating" in Korean - 도금([do-geum/plating])

What does "도금([do-geum/plating])" mean?

도금([do-geum/plating]) means "plating". It is coating metal surface with thin layer of gold, nickel, chrome to make shiny, rust-proof.

Casual: 도금이야 (do-geum-i-ya)
Polite: 도금해요 (do-geum-hae-yo)
Formal: 도금입니다 (do-geum-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

도금([do-geum/plating]) = 도 + 금 (coat + metal)
도금작업([do-geum-ja-geop/plating work]) = 도금 + 작업 (plating + work)
도금액([do-geum-aek/plating solution]) = 도금 + 액 (plating + liquid)
도금두께([do-geum-du-kke/plating thickness]) = 도금 + 두께 (plating + thickness)
도금불량([do-geum-bul-ryang/plating defect]) = 도금 + 불량 (plating + defect)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Plating: “도금 했어요? (do-geum hae-sseo-yo?)”
2. Solution: 도금액([do-geum-aek/plating solution]) chemical in tank for coating.
3. Defect: 도금불량([do-geum-bul-ryang/plating defect]) peeling, spot, uneven color.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 도금 했어요? A: do-geum hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you do plating?

B: 네, 했어요. B: ne, hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, plated.

A: 도금 어떻게 해요? A: do-geum eo-ttae-ke hae-yo?

A: How to do plating?

B: 전기로 해요. B: jeon-gi-ro hae-yo.

B: By electricity. - 도금([do-geum/plating])

A: 도금액 농도 어때요? A: do-geum-aek nong-do eo-ttae-yo?

A: How is plating solution concentration?

B: 정상이에요. B: jeong-sang-i-e-yo.

B: Normal.

A: 도금액 온도 어때요? A: do-geum-aek on-do eo-ttae-yo?

A: How is plating solution temp?

B: 50도예요. B: 50 degrees.

B: 50 degrees. - 도금액([do-geum-aek/plating solution])

A: 도금불량 있어요? A: do-geum-bul-ryang i-sseo-yo?

A: Is there plating defect?

B: 네, 있어요. B: ne, i-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, there is defect.

A: 도금불량 왜 생겼어요? A: do-geum-bul-ryang wae saeng-gyeo-sseo-yo?

A: Why plating defect occurred?

B: 전류가 높아서요. B: jeon-ryu-ga nop-a-seo-yo.

B: Current too high. - 도금불량([do-geum-bul-ryang/plating defect])
