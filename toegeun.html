Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When do Koreans say "leave work" — 퇴근 (toe-geun)?

Koreans use "퇴근 (toe-geun)" when finishing a shift and leaving the workplace — a key EPS-TOPIK term for daily attendance and work schedule.

Q2. Is "퇴근이에요 (toe-geun-i-e-yo)" polite enough?

Yes. "퇴근이에요 (toe-geun-i-e-yo)" is polite and natural. You can also say "퇴근이야 (toe-geun-i-ya)" casually with close colleagues.

Q3. What should I say in a formal setting?

You can say "I am leaving work now" — 지금 퇴근입니다 (ji-geum toe-geun-im-ni-da) — when speaking to a manager or during workplace communication.

📘 EPS-TOPIK Learn More: 퇴근 (toe-geun / Leave Work)

What does 퇴근 (toe-geun) mean?

퇴근 (toe-geun) means “leave work” or “get off work.” It is the opposite of 출근 (chul-geun / go to work) and is one of the most important attendance words in the EPS-TOPIK exam and real Korean factory life.

When to Use It

1. End of shift: “지금 퇴근이에요 (ji-geum toe-geun-i-e-yo)” (I’m leaving work now).
2. Talking about schedule: “퇴근 시간 (toe-geun si-gan)이 몇 시예요?” (What time do you finish work?).
3. Related attendance words: 지각 (ji-gak / being late), 결근 (gyeol-geun / absence), 조퇴 (jo-toe / leave early), 야근 (ya-geun / overtime).

Similar Expressions

• 출근 (chul-geun) – Go to work (the opposite of 퇴근 (toe-geun))
• 조퇴 (jo-toe) – Leave work early
• 야근 (ya-geun) – Work late / night overtime
• 퇴근카드 (toe-geun-ka-deu) – Time card for leaving

Real-Life Examples (EPS-TOPIK Style)

오늘 6시에 퇴근해요. o-neul yeo-seot-si-e toe-geun-hae-yo.

I leave work at 6 today.

퇴근 시간이 되면 알려 주세요. toe-geun si-gan-i doe-myeon al-lyeo ju-se-yo.

Please tell me when it’s time to leave work.

어제 야근해서 늦게 퇴근했어. eo-je ya-geun-hae-seo neut-ge toe-geun-hae-sseo.

I worked overtime yesterday, so I left late. (Casual)

매일 정시에 퇴근합니다. mae-il jeong-si-e toe-geun-ham-ni-da.

I leave work on time every day. (Formal)
