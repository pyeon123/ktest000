Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Leave work"?

퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]) means finishing work and going home from factory, opposite of 출근([chul-geun/go to work]).

Q2. What is "Leaving time" and "Way home"?

퇴근시간([toe-geun-si-gan/leaving time]) is official time to leave, 퇴근길([toe-geun-gil/way home]) is road from work to home.

Q3. What is "On-time leave" and "Night overtime"?

정시퇴근([jeong-si-toe-geun/on-time leave]) is leaving exactly on time, 야근([ya-geun/night overtime]) is working late after 퇴근시간([toe-geun-si-gan/leaving time]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Leave Work" in Korean - 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work])

What does "퇴근([toe-geun/leave work])" mean?

퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]) means "leave work" or "go home from work". Opposite of 출근([chul-geun/go to work]).

Casual: 퇴근이야 (toe-geun-i-ya)
Polite: 퇴근이에요 (toe-geun-i-e-yo)
Formal: 퇴근입니다 (toe-geun-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

퇴근([toe-geun/leave work]) = 퇴([toe/leaving]) + 근([geun/work]). 퇴근시간([toe-geun-si-gan/leaving time]) = 퇴근 + 시간, 퇴근길([toe-geun-gil/way home]) = 퇴근 + 길, 정시퇴근([jeong-si-toe-geun/on-time leave]) = 정시([jeong-si/on time]) + 퇴근, 야근([ya-geun/night overtime]) = 야([ya/night]) + 근([geun/work]).

When to Use It

1. Finishing work: "오늘 퇴근해요? (o-neul toe-geun-hae-yo?)"
2. Time & road: Talking about 퇴근시간([toe-geun-si-gan/leaving time]) and 퇴근길([toe-geun-gil/way home]) crowded.
3. Overtime: If 잔업([jan-eop/extra work]) or 야근([ya-geun/night overtime]) exists, 퇴근 is late.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue

A: 오늘 퇴근해요? A: o-neul toe-geun-hae-yo?

A: Do you leave work today?

B: 네, 6시에 퇴근해요. B: ne, yeo-seot-si-e toe-geun-hae-yo.

B: Yes, I leave at 6.

A: 몇 시에 퇴근해요? A: myeot si-e toe-geun-hae-yo?

A: What time do you leave work?

B: 6시에 퇴근해요. B: yeo-seot-si-e toe-geun-hae-yo.

B: I leave at 6 o'clock. - 퇴근([toe-geun/leave work])

A: 퇴근시간이 몇 시예요? A: toe-geun-si-gan-i myeot si-ye-yo?

A: What time is leaving time?

B: 6시예요. B: yeo-seot-si-ye-yo.

B: It's 6 o'clock.

A: 퇴근시간 지켜요? A: toe-geun-si-gan ji-kyeo-yo?

A: Do you keep leaving time?

B: 네, 6시에 퇴근해요. B: ne, yeo-seot-si-e toe-geun-hae-yo.

B: Yes, I leave at 6. - 퇴근시간([toe-geun-si-gan/leaving time])

A: 퇴근길 막혀요? A: toe-geun-gil mak-hyeo-yo?

A: Is way home crowded?

B: 네, 많이 막혀요. B: ne, man-i mak-hyeo-yo.

B: Yes, very crowded.

A: 퇴근길 얼마나 걸려요? A: toe-geun-gil eol-ma-na geol-lyeo-yo?

A: How long is way home?

B: 40분 걸려요. B: ma-heun-bun geol-lyeo-yo.

B: It takes 40 minutes. - 퇴근길([toe-geun-gil/way home])

A: 오늘 야근 있어요? A: o-neul ya-geun i-sseo-yo?

A: Do you have night overtime today?

B: 네, 야근 2시간 있어요. B: ne, ya-geun du si-gan i-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, 2 hours night overtime.

A: 야근 끝나고 몇 시에 퇴근해요? A: ya-geun kkeut-na-go myeot si-e toe-geun-hae-yo?

A: What time do you leave after night overtime?

B: 9시에 퇴근해요. B: a-hop-si-e toe-geun-hae-yo.

B: I leave at 9. - 야근([ya-geun/night overtime])
