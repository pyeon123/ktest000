Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Leave work"?

퇴근 (toe-geun) leave work is the standard word for finishing your shift and leaving the workplace.

Q2. What is "Overtime" in Korean?

야근 (ya-geun) overtime refers to working late into the night beyond your regular shift.

Q3. How do you say "Company dinner" and "Way home from work"?

회식 (hoe-sik) company dinner and 퇴근길 (toe-geun-gil) way home from work are common words used in daily workplace life.

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Leave Work" in Korean

What does "퇴근 (toe-geun)" mean?

퇴근 (toe-geun) means "leave work" or "clock out." It is the standard EPS-TOPIK word for finishing a shift and heading home.

Casual: 퇴근이야 (toe-geun-i-ya) - Friends/family
Polite: 퇴근이에요 (toe-geun-i-e-yo) - Standard everyday
Formal: 퇴근입니다 (toe-geun-im-ni-da) - Official/respectful

Grammar Breakdown

퇴근 (toe-geun) is made of two parts — 퇴 (leave/retreat) + 근 (work/duty). It is used across all Korean workplaces to describe finishing a shift.

When to Use It

1. Talking about your schedule: "몇 시에 퇴근해요? (myeot si-e toe-geun-hae-yo)" (What time do you get off work?).
2. Explaining a long day: Mentioning 야근 (ya-geun) overtime before you finally 퇴근.
3. Daily routine: Talking about 회식 (hoe-sik) company dinner or 퇴근길 (toe-geun-gil) way home from work.

Real-Life Examples

오늘 몇 시에 퇴근해요? o-neul myeot si-e toe-geun-hae-yo?

What time do you get off work today?

오늘 야근해야 돼요. o-neul ya-geun-hae-ya dwae-yo.

I have to work overtime today.

퇴근길이 너무 막혀요. toe-geun-gi-ri neo-mu ma-kyeo-yo.

The way home from work is very congested.

Similar Expressions

• 출근 (chul-geun) go to work
• 야근 (ya-geun) overtime
• 칼퇴근 (kal-toe-geun) leaving right on time
• 회식 (hoe-sik) company dinner
