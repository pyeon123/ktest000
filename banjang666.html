Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do Koreans say "Foreman"?

반장([ban-jang/foreman]) is a team leader who leads small team of workers, checks work quality, gives daily instructions.

Q2. What is "Group leader"?

조장([jo-jang/group leader]) is a small group leader, usually leads 3-5 workers in same line or process.

Q3. How to call foreman politely?

반장님([ban-jang-nim/foreman polite]) is polite form of 반장([ban-jang/foreman]) by adding 님([nim/Mr.]). Workers should call 반장님([ban-jang-nim/foreman polite]).

EPS TOPIK Learn More: How to Say "Foreman" in Korean - 반장([ban-jang/foreman])

What does "반장([ban-jang/foreman])" mean?

반장([ban-jang/foreman]) means "foreman". It is a team leader who leads small team, checks work, gives instructions.

Casual: 반장이야 (ban-jang-i-ya)
Polite: 반장이에요 (ban-jang-i-e-yo)
Formal: 반장입니다 (ban-jang-im-ni-da)

Grammar Breakdown

반장([ban-jang/foreman]) = 반 + 장 (team + leader)
조장([jo-jang/group leader]) = 조 + 장 (group + leader)
작업반장([ja-geop-ban-jang/work team foreman]) = 작업 + 반장 (work + foreman)
생산반장([saeng-san-ban-jang/production foreman]) = 생산 + 반장 (production + foreman)
반장님([ban-jang-nim/foreman polite]) = 반장 + 님 (foreman + Mr.)

When to Use It (EPS-TOPIK Style)

1. Workplace: “반장이 누구예요? (ban-jang-i nu-gu-ye-yo?)”
2. Group: 조장([jo-jang/group leader]) leads 3-5 workers.
3. Polite: Call 반장님([ban-jang-nim/foreman polite]) with 님 to show respect.

Real-Life Examples - A-B-A-B Dialogue (EPS-TOPIK)

A: 반장이 누구예요? A: ban-jang-i nu-gu-ye-yo?

A: Who is foreman?

B: 김 반장이에요. B: gim ban-jang-i-e-yo.

B: It is Foreman Kim.

A: 반장님께 말했어요? A: ban-jang-nim-kke mal-hae-sseo-yo?

A: Did you tell foreman?

B: 네, 말했어요. B: ne, mal-hae-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I told him. - 반장([ban-jang/foreman])

A: 조장이 누구예요? A: jo-jang-i nu-gu-ye-yo?

A: Who is group leader?

B: 박 조장이에요. B: bak jo-jang-i-e-yo.

B: It is Group Leader Park.

A: 조장님께 물어봤어요? A: jo-jang-nim-kke mul-eo-bwa-sseo-yo?

A: Did you ask group leader?

B: 네, 물어봤어요. B: ne, mul-eo-bwa-sseo-yo.

B: Yes, I asked. - 조장([jo-jang/group leader])

A: 반장님 계세요? A: ban-jang-nim gye-se-yo?

A: Is foreman there?

B: 네, 계세요. B: ne, gye-se-yo.

B: Yes, he is.

A: 반장님, 도와주세요. A: ban-jang-nim, do-wa-ju-se-yo.

A: Foreman, please help me.

B: 네, 알겠습니다. B: ne, al-get-seum-ni-da.

B: Yes, I understand. - 반장님([ban-jang-nim/foreman polite])
