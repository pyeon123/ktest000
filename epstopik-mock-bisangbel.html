Q1
비상벨은 어디에 있습니까?
(bisangbeleun eodi-e itseumnikka?)
Where is the emergency bell?
Script (스크립트)

여자: 비상벨이 어디에 있어요? 남자: 계단 옆에 있어요.

Woman: bisangbeli eodi-e isseoyo? Man: gyedan yeope isseoyo.

Woman: Where is the emergency bell? Man: It is next to the stairs.

📚 Study “emergency bell” (비상벨)
Q2
비상벨은 어떻게 작동합니까?
(bisangbeleun eotteoke jakdonghamnikka?)
How does the emergency bell work?
Script (스크립트)

남자: 비상벨은 어떻게 작동해요? 여자: 빨간 버튼을 세게 누르면 돼요.

Man: bisangbeleun eotteoke jakdonghaeyo? Woman: ppalgan beoteuneul sege nureumyeon dwaeyo.

Man: How does the emergency bell work? Woman: Just press the red button hard.

📚 Study “emergency bell button” (비상벨 버튼)
Q3
소방 점검은 몇 시입니까?
(sobang jeomgeomeun myeot siimnikka?)
What time is the fire safety inspection?
Script (스크립트)

여자: 소방 점검은 언제예요? 남자: 내일 오전 열 시예요.

Woman: sobang jeomgeomeun eonjeyeyo? Man: naeil ojeon yeol siyeyo.

Woman: When is the fire safety inspection? Man: Tomorrow at 10 AM.

📚 Study “safety inspection” (안전 점검)
Q4
여자가 먼저 확인하라고 한 것은 무엇입니까?
(yeojaga meonjeo hwaginhara-go han geoseun mueosimnikka?)
What did the woman ask the man to check first?
Script (스크립트)

남자: 기계에서 소리가 안 나요. 여자: 먼저 전원이 켜져 있는지 확인해 보세요.

Man: gigyeeseo soriga an nayo. Woman: meonjeo jeonwoni kyeojyeo itneunji hwaginhae boseyo.

Man: The machine is not making any sound. Woman: First, check whether the power is on.

📚 Study “switch” (스위치)
Q5
화재가 났을 때 해야 할 행동으로 맞는 것은?
(hwajaega naesseul ttae haeya hal haengdongeuro matneun geoseun?)
Which action is correct when there is a fire?
Script (스크립트)

남자: 화재가 나면 어떻게 해요? 여자: 비상벨을 누르고 빨리 밖으로 대피하세요.

Man: hwajaega namyeon eotteoke haeyo? Woman: bisangbeleul nureugo ppalli bakkeuro daepihaseyo.

Man: What should you do in a fire? Woman: Press the emergency bell and evacuate outside quickly.

📚 Study “evacuation” (대피)
Q6
안전모는 어디에 있습니까?
(anjeonmoneun eodi-e itseumnikka?)
Where is the safety helmet?
Script (스크립트)

여자: 안전모는 어디에 있어요? 남자: 창고 안 선반 위에 있어요.

Woman: anjeonmoneun eodi-e isseoyo? Man: changgo an seonban wi-e isseoyo.

Woman: Where is the safety helmet? Man: It is on the shelf inside the warehouse.

📚 Study “safety helmet” (안전모)

EPS-TOPIK Study Guide

What is EPS-TOPIK?

EPS-TOPIK is the Korean language proficiency test for foreign workers who want to work in Korea under the Employment Permit System. For test information and the latest schedule, check HRD Korea (hrd.go.kr) and the Employment Permit System website (eps.go.kr).

How to study safety vocabulary

Learn workplace words such as 비상벨 (bisangbel, emergency bell), 비상구 (bisanggu, emergency exit), and 소화기 (sohwagi, fire extinguisher) together with a full sentence. Practice question and answer pairs such as "Where is it?" and "How do you use it?" to prepare for the listening section.

Listening and reading tips

Listening questions often ask about place, time, or method. For reading, look at the sentences before and after the blank to find the answer. After each mistake, read the script and explanation again.
