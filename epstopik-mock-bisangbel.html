Frequently Asked Questions

What should you do first when a fire breaks out?

Call 119, press the 비상벨 (emergency bell) if there is one, warn people nearby, and evacuate by the 계단 (stairs). Do not use the elevator.

Should you put water on a cooking oil fire?

No. Water can make an oil fire spread. Cover the pan with a 뚜껑 (lid) and turn off the gas if it is safe to do so.

EPS-TOPIK Study Guide

What is EPS-TOPIK?

EPS-TOPIK is the Korean language proficiency test for foreign workers who want to work in Korea under the Employment Permit System. For test information and the latest schedule, check HRD Korea (hrd.go.kr) and the Employment Permit System website (eps.go.kr).

How to study fire safety vocabulary

Learn words such as 화재 (fire), 소화기 (fire extinguisher), and 대피 (evacuation) together with a full sentence. Practice question and answer pairs such as "Where is it?" and "What should I do?"

Listening and reading tips

Listening questions often ask about place, time, or the action a speaker recommends. For reading, look at the sentences before and after the blank. After each mistake, read the script and explanation again.
