Frequently Asked Questions

What should you do first if you smell gas in the dormitory?

Open the window, do not turn on any lights or electrical devices, close the gas valve if it is safe, leave the room, and tell the manager right away. In Korean, this is 관리자에게 알리세요 (tell the manager).

Where do you put food waste in a Korean dormitory?

Food waste usually goes into a separate 음식물 쓰레기 (food waste) bin. Recyclables such as plastic bottles and vinyl go into recycling bins on the scheduled day.

EPS-TOPIK Study Guide

What is EPS-TOPIK?

EPS-TOPIK is the Korean language proficiency test for foreign workers who want to work in Korea under the Employment Permit System. For test information and the latest schedule, check HRD Korea (hrd.go.kr) and the Employment Permit System website (eps.go.kr).

How to study dormitory vocabulary

Learn daily life words such as 가스 (gas), 밸브 (valve), and 쓰레기 (waste) together with a full sentence. Practice pairs such as "Where is it?" and "What should I do?" to prepare for the listening section.

Listening and reading tips

Listening questions often ask about place, time, or the action a speaker recommends. For reading, look at the sentences before and after the blank. After each mistake, read the script and explanation again.
